\section{VO: valuation plans}
\label{sec:vo}\label{sec:nf-vo}

\paragraph{Grammar.} VO extends a complete CQ$_{\rm NF}$ program with a schedule.
\begin{lstlisting}
VO   ::= CQProgram valuation [Evidence] [OrderingInputs] { Step* }
Evidence ::= evidence from "provenance" { (count R = n; | key R [col, ...];)* }
OrderingInputs ::= ordering(seed = Bool, delta = none | Index,
    driver = WrittenOrder | DeltaFirst, permutation = [Index, ...],
    shared_demand = none | [Relation(ColumnIndex, ...), ...])
Step ::= R@path(x, _, ...); | !R(Arg, ...); | x = y; | where Constraint;
       | if Expr; | let Pat [: Type] = Expr;
       | for Pat in Expr;
       | reduce R@path with S@path(...);
       | restrict R@path where Constraint;
       | block @path(columns) Placement { Step* } (| { Step* })+;
       | block @path(columns) Placement { Step* } (else { Step* })+;
Placement ::= hoisted | correlated
\end{lstlisting}
Number all NF body clauses $0,\ldots,m-1$; a path \code{R@c.j.k} names the fixed
occurrence of clause $c$, extended by branch/clause pairs per nesting level.
A subatom lists the occurrence's currently bound columns and \code{_} elsewhere.
Equalities and constraints use internal relational syntax: \code{where} prefixes the existing \code{=}, \code{between}, \code{in \{...\}}, \code{and}/\code{or} forms; raw \code{x = y;} remains an identity step.
Optional ordering inputs are carried unchanged from CQ and printed as recorded.
Delta and permutation use original Datalog body identities, not positions
renumbered by CQ normalization. Shared demand is checked input only in step 1,
not a ranking or index-reuse claim. The executable VO contract independently
checks I1--I3 (VO-Order, Section~\ref{sec:planning-order}) in every scope;
Written and authored schedules are not exempt. CQ occurrence-coverage proof
references used by correspondence checks are not executable VOs.

\paragraph{Contract.} $\mathsf{Schedule}(t,A)$ checks:
(1)~each subatom names its relation, clause, arity and original columns; dropped
columns are unbound; every atom's full correlated row is eventually checked;
(2)~each identity, constraint and negation appears exactly once, after its
variables bind, and binds nothing; negation preserves its source atom; a
written predicate restricting some occurrence (6) may omit its step;
(3)~each Rust let, generator and guard appears exactly once with unchanged
expression, pattern, annotation and lexical bindings, after all its free
variables bind. Lexically opaque expressions require their whole environment;
there is no additional written-order or relational-predecessor restriction;
(4)~a reduction names a different atom or Rust-free hoisted block and lists
exactly the shared equality classes at its support's column positions; it
precedes the target's first subatom, except that a target already read to
supply a finite block domain restricts the retained prefix instead; a
reduction naming a hoisted block that takes a finite prefix domain follows
that block's free columns;
no Rust guard, scalar bind, generator or negation is a reduction target or support;
the reduction graph need not be acyclic;
(5)~blocks recursively check kind, paths, interface coverage, arm schedules and
the required placement field. Hoisted blocks contain no Rust steps, and one
taking a finite prefix domain (an arm lacks a generator for a free column)
follows its free columns as provided by the scope (a correlated arm's prefix,
a hoisted arm's finite domain, or the scope's own earlier steps; a hoisted
arm without a finite domain provides none of its free columns up front); correlated
blocks require every free prefix variable. Repeated reads retain placement;
(6)~a restriction (WF-Restrict) names a positive atom occurrence of its scope,
carries a written relational predicate other than a variable identity (a
literal equality in constraint form) or one of its E-Domain implied domains,
uses only columns of that occurrence, and precedes every other step of the
scope. It binds nothing; it filters the occurrence's own relation, so
$\sigma_P(R)\bowtie p=\sigma_P(R\bowtie p)$ since $\vars(P)\subseteq\vars(R)$.
Let $B$ be the visible bindings and $P\subseteq B$ the columns provided by
the scope's finite seed or preceding steps. A hoisted arm without a finite
domain starts with $P=\emptyset$, even when its free columns are visible in $B$.
Every step extends $P$ by its provided columns.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{D-Constraint}{\vars(C)\subseteq B}
 {\mathsf{requires}(C)=\vars(C),\quad\mathsf{provides}(C)=\emptyset}

\Rule{WF-VO-Rust}{\forall i\text{ a Rust step}.\ \fv(e_i)\cap Q\subseteq B_i\\
 \mathsf{PlacedOnce}(i)\quad\mathsf{UnchangedRustPayload}(i)}
 {\mathsf{RustSchedule}(t)\Downarrow\ok}

\Rule{WF-BlockPlacement}{b.placement\in\{\mathsf{hoisted},\mathsf{correlated}\}\\
 b.placement=\mathsf{hoisted}\Longrightarrow\neg\mathsf{Rust}(b)\\
 b.placement=\mathsf{hoisted}\land\mathsf{needsDomain}(b)\Longrightarrow F_b\subseteq P
  \text{ at }b\text{ and at every reduction naming }b\\
 b.placement=\mathsf{correlated}\Longrightarrow F_b\subseteq B\\
 \mathsf{RepeatedReadsAgree}(b.placement)\quad
 \mathsf{ReductionOperands}(t)\cap\mathsf{CorrelatedBlocks}(t)=\emptyset}
 {\mathsf{BlockPlacement}(b,B,P,t)\Downarrow\ok}

\Rule{WF-Restrict}{\mathsf{restrict}\ R@i\ \mathsf{where}\ P\quad c_i=R(\bar x)\text{ positive}\\
 \emptyset\ne\vars(P)\subseteq\bar x\quad
 P\in\mathsf{written}\cup\bigcup_{C\in\mathsf{written}}\mathsf{impliedDomains}(C)\\
 \text{no non-restriction step precedes it in its scope}}
 {\mathsf{Restriction}(t,i,P)\Downarrow\ok}

\Rule{WF-Evidence}{\mathcal{E}.provenance\ne\epsilon\quad
 \forall f\in\mathcal{E}.\ \mathsf{relation}(f)\in\mathsf{inputs}(t.cq)\\
 \le 1\ \mathsf{count}\text{ per relation}\quad
 \text{keys nonempty, distinct, of declared columns}}
 {\mathsf{Evidence}(t.cq,\mathcal{E})\Downarrow\ok}

\Rule{WF-VO}{\mathsf{WF}_{NF}(t.cq)\quad\mathsf{Schedule}(t,A)\\
 t.evidence\text{ present}\Rightarrow\mathsf{Evidence}(t.cq,t.evidence)\Downarrow\ok}
 {\mathsf{WF}_{VO}(t)\Downarrow A}
\end{mathpar}

\paragraph{Meaning.} Compose the steps in schedule order under
Section~\ref{sec:denotation}: a subatom extends valuations with matching
projected rows, a reduction is a relational semijoin on its target, a
restriction selects its occurrence's relation before any use, negation
keeps a valuation exactly when no input row matches (CQ-Neg, RL-Neg), and Rust
steps are as in CQ. By invariants (1)--(6) a schedule preserves its CQ under
the totality and representation premises of Section~\ref{sec:premises}:
every Rust step retains its free-variable bindings and original expression,
every predicate has its relational denotation, and every atom's full row is
checked. Motion does not depend on Rust syntax or host type classification.

\paragraph{Recorded decisions.} Clause order, split granularity (Coarse: one full
subatom per atom; Fine: at most one new variable per subatom), semijoin
reductions, occurrence restrictions (where each relational predicate runs),
and block placement (hoisted
derived relation or per-row arms).
Recorded evidence (row
counts and asserted keys with their provenance) is the input of the evidence
ordering O-EvidenceLe; it never changes the meaning of a schedule.

\paragraph{Rewrite check.} Unchanged CQ; the non-reduction step erasures are equal
recursively; existing reductions are kept and added reductions satisfy the
target contract. Non-reduction steps are not
changed by a rewrite.

\section{Context facts: assume--guarantee between compilers}
\label{sec:context}

Every pass proves refinement conditionally. The premises of
Section~\ref{sec:premises} are global and unverifiable; the facts of this
section are \emph{local}: a program states a fact about its context that it
cannot establish itself, a lower contract relaxes an obligation under that
fact, and whoever closes the context must discharge it. Nothing here changes
the written-order denotation $T X=\bot+\operatorname{Set}X$ or the relation
model; a context fact refines within the fixed meaning. Multiplicities,
result order and annotation semirings are not context facts: they change the
model and belong to a dialect with a checked translation
(Section~\ref{sec:datalog-languages}).

\paragraph{Grammar.} Context facts are recorded in Physical and carried by
containment into Structures and Sequence.
\begin{lstlisting}
Physical ::= physical { Decl* Fact* Def* return [canon] r as Head; }
Fact     ::= host_set R [discharged];
           | consumer canonical;
\end{lstlisting}
A fact without \code{discharged} is \emph{certified}: the generated program
establishes it at runtime before the query executes (\textsc{IF-HostSet}).
A fact marked \code{discharged} is established by the compiler that invoked
this pass; it costs nothing at runtime and is admissible only under
\textsc{CTX-Discharge} below. \code{consumer canonical} has no certified
form: no program can test what its consumer will do.

\paragraph{Meaning of a program under facts.} Let $\Gamma$ be the recorded
facts. The judgment $\Gamma\vdash P$ reads: $P$ denotes
$\llbracket P\rrbracket$ in every context satisfying $\Gamma$. For
$\Gamma=\emptyset$ this is the unconditional meaning used everywhere else.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{CTX-HostSet}{\code{host\_set R}\in\Gamma}
 {\alpha(R)\text{ is a set: }\mathsf{fullRows}(R)\text{ has no duplicate}}

\Rule{CTX-Consumer}{\code{consumer canonical}\in\Gamma\quad
 \kappa\text{ consumes the returned stream}}
 {\kappa(B)=\kappa(\mathrm{supp}(B))\text{ for every bag }B}
\end{mathpar}
Under \textsc{CTX-Consumer} the returned stream may repeat witnesses: the
program's guarantee at the return is $\mathrm{supp}$-equality, and the set
boundary is owned by $\kappa$. This is the statement, already present in
Section~\ref{sec:incremental} (INC-Supp), that a consumer may own the final
canonical boundary only when multiplicity is unobservable there.

\paragraph{What a fact relaxes.} Exactly two obligations consult $\Gamma$;
every other contract is unchanged.
\textsc{PH-HostSet} (Section~\ref{sec:physical}) and:
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{CK-Return}{\mathsf{unique}(t.returned)\ \vee\
 \code{return canon}\in t\ \vee\ \code{consumer canonical}\in\Gamma}
 {\text{the returned boundary of }t\text{ is admissible}}
\end{mathpar}
\textsc{PH-HostSet} seeds the uniqueness lattice at a host rename; joins
combine keys and projections keep a key they retain, so a hole whose head
keeps the inherited key needs no \code{canon} at all. \textsc{CK-Return}
replaces the third premise of \textsc{CK-Physical} and the corresponding
Structures rule: with \code{consumer canonical} lowering records no
\code{return canon}, Structures records no head dedup provider, and the
printers emit no local set. Internal \code{canon} boundaries (union arms,
noninjective computed heads inside the plan) are untouched: they serve
intermediate joins, not the result's set-ness.

\paragraph{Discharge.} A fact is sound only if someone establishes it.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{CTX-Certify}{\code{host\_set R}\in\Gamma\text{ certified}\quad
 \mathsf{certify}(R)\Downarrow\ok\ (\textsc{IF-HostSet})}
 {\text{the executing program establishes CTX-HostSet}}

\Rule{CTX-Discharge}{\varphi\in\Gamma\text{ discharged}\quad
 \varphi\in\mathsf{facts}(args)\quad
 \mathsf{check}_C(\text{invoking program})\text{ proves }\varphi}
 {\text{the invoking compiler }C\text{ establishes }\varphi}

\Rule{CTX-Entry}{\code{mini\_linq::compile!}\{\ldots\}\text{ starts }\pi\quad
 \Gamma\text{ contains a discharged fact}}
 {\text{rejected: a top-level entry has no context to discharge it}}
\end{mathpar}
A discharged fact enters a pass only through its argument packet
(\textsc{ST-Next}: passes receive no configuration beyond their arguments),
so the recorded program names the fact and the pass that wrote it names the
packet. The invoking compiler $C$ is any language that compiles to CQ and
owns a checker: its correspondence check must prove $\varphi$ structurally,
never by rerunning its own producer. \textsc{CTX-Entry} makes the
unconditional case the default: every existing program has
$\Gamma=\emptyset$, and a user who writes a discharged fact by hand at the
top level is refused rather than trusted.

\begin{theorem}[Closed context]
If $\Gamma\vdash P$, $C$ discharges every discharged fact of $\Gamma$, and
the executing program certifies every certified fact, then $C[P]$ refines the
unconditional meaning of $C[P']$ where $P'$ is $P$ with $\Gamma=\emptyset$
and the relaxed boundaries restored.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\textsc{PH-HostSet}: a duplicate-free rename has its heading as key, so every
key the lattice derives from it is a key of the unconditional program's
canonical result; dropping a \code{canon} implied by such a key keeps the
set. \textsc{CK-Return}: the returned bag $B$ satisfies
$\mathrm{supp}(B)=\llbracket P'\rrbracket$ by the unchanged pass
refinements modulo $\mathrm{supp}$, and $\kappa(B)=\kappa(\mathrm{supp}(B))$
by \textsc{CTX-Consumer}. Panics are unchanged: no relaxation adds a step.
Compose with Theorem~\ref{thm:composition}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Pass refinement under a consumer fact.} For a pass $f:s\mapsto t$
on a program with \code{consumer canonical}, the obligation at the returned
result is $\mathrm{supp}(\llbracket s\rrbracket)=\mathrm{supp}(\llbracket
t\rrbracket)$ and no new $\bot$; elsewhere it is unchanged. Reductions,
disjoint-union rewrites and dedup retyping already satisfy this: none
changes a support.

\paragraph{Instances.} The following languages compile to CQ and may
discharge facts; each names the checker that proves them.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Invoking compiler & Fact & Proof obligation of its checker\\
\midrule
Datalog Storage & \code{host\_set H discharged} & $H$ is a canonical EDB provider, an IDB \code{all}/\code{delta} fed only through admission, or a layout maintained from them\\
Datalog Round & \code{consumer canonical} & the hole is listed in exactly one \code{admit R from [..]} with $R$ its head (DL-Admit-Owner)\\
Recursion extension & both & delta is \code{Answers}; results enter \code{fixpoint\_seen}\\
Aggregate, negation extensions & \code{consumer canonical} & results enter the extension's own set state\\
Top-level \code{mini\_linq!} & \code{host\_set R} certified only & \textsc{IF-HostSet}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Deferred, as further facts of the same shape: \code{host\_key R [c..]}
(projection keeps a declared key; TPC-H primary keys), \code{consumer
exists} (first witness, lawful early stop), \code{guard total} (a
compiler-generated guard certified total, generalizing \code{\#[total]}),
and the terminal guarantee \code{canonical-return} that replaces path-name
matching in extension contexts (\textsc{EXT-SetFold}).

\paragraph{Multi-stage control.} Facts are the interface between stages of a
stacked compiler: the upper stage decides what it will guarantee about inputs
and consumers, records it in the lower program it emits, and the lower stage
compiles against that interface without knowing the upper language. The
lower program remains a complete, checkable text; the only trace of the
upper stage is $\Gamma$, and \textsc{CTX-Entry} ensures it cannot be run
without that stage.

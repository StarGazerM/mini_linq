\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related-work}
AHV supplies the standard semantics of valuations, named algebra, join trees
and acyclic queries~\cite{ahv}; the course notes connect them to executable
join planning~\cite{notes}. MiniLinq's occurrence labels preserve repeated
uses and positional maps without changing the classical graph notions.
Its greedy arity policy makes no new join-complexity claim.

Language-integrated-query calculi justify normalization into specified target
fragments~\cite{host-linq,host-effective}. Here native Rust can remain residual
through relational transformations, so its eventual binding and execution
context must also be justified. Multi-language semantics provides the relevant
boundary discipline~\cite{host-multilanguage}. Syn parsing itself establishes
neither that semantic adequacy nor hygienic binding preservation~\cite{host-hygiene}.

Nanopass organizes compilation around small languages and focused
transformations~\cite{paper-nanopass}. MiniLinq additionally exposes lower
macro entries and emits Rust with local quotations. Its replacement theorem
therefore assumes contextual adequacy, following the distinction between
whole-program and compositional compiler correctness~\cite{paper-ccc}.

Ascent BYODS formalizes custom representations and their interaction with
evaluation~\cite{paper-byods}. We expose the intervening algorithm, access and
observation contracts needed for substitution. User-defined predicate
optimization already shows why placement depends on both dependencies and
costs~\cite{paper-udp}. Push/pull fusion and multiway joins supply alternative
execution techniques~\cite{paper-pushpull,paper-lftj}; they require the
appropriate compiler segment to change. The present artifact demonstrates
existing pass and quotation reuse, leaving those additional backends unimplemented.

\section{Validation}
\label{sec:validation}\label{sec:evaluation}
Acceptance needs three independent kinds of evidence:
$\mathsf{RuleDerivation}\land\mathsf{ExpectedExpansion}\land\mathsf{SemanticAgreement}$.
Passing one never substitutes for another.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{.27\linewidth}p{.65\linewidth}}
\toprule
Kind & What is run, and what it establishes\\
\midrule
Structural checks & Every contract and correspondence rule above, with mutation
tests per obligation (\code{languages/*/tests}, \code{passes/*/tests}). Establishes
the structural premises, not Rust semantics.\\
Expansion fixtures & Authored macro outputs compared up to whitespace and spans.
Establishes that decisions are recorded where claimed.\\
Storage laws & Abstraction functions of indexes, membership and ordered cursors
against an independent bag oracle (\code{runtime/cq-storage/tests}).\\
Answer oracles & \code{testkit/cq-testkit}: an independent relational semantics
and a written-order Rust reference; the corpus runs every planner, rewrite, host
type and printer configuration.\\
Differential fuzzing & Seeded queries with constants, explicit relational
predicates, opaque pure-total Rust, blocks, duplicates and empty relations.
Reference comparisons require answer equality under the premises; a panic is
a contract violation rather than an accepted refinement case.\\
TPC-derived kernels & Exact answer comparison with DuckDB at scale factors 0.1
and 1; timing and counters are reported, never asserted.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
None of this proves the runtime trait laws for arbitrary user types, adequacy of
Rust resolution and ownership, or any complexity bound.

\paragraph{Related work.}
AHV gives valuations, named algebra, join trees and acyclicity~\cite{ahv}; the
course notes connect them to executable planning~\cite{notes}.
Language-integrated query calculi justify normalization to a target
fragment~\cite{host-linq,host-effective}; residual Rust needs binding and context
adequacy as in multi-language semantics~\cite{host-multilanguage}, which Syn
parsing does not supply~\cite{host-hygiene}. The pass structure follows
nanopass~\cite{paper-nanopass} and compositional compiler
correctness~\cite{paper-ccc}. Ascent BYODS formalizes custom
representations~\cite{paper-byods}; predicate placement relates to
dependencies and cost~\cite{paper-udp}. Push/pull fusion and LFTJ complexity
results~\cite{paper-pushpull,paper-lftj} are not established here.

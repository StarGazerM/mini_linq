\section{Current CQ-to-RA administrative algorithms}
\label{sec:planning}
D-analysis chooses graph or prefix transitions. Incidence and a GYO join tree
constrain command selection; commands emit RA and witness binary assembly order.
\textsc{Apply} checks a command before updating state and appending its trace
entry. A failed command or nested check aborts the enclosing algorithm.
These judgments certify the specified strategy families. Eager selection,
exact trimming and phase order restrict those families beyond semantic
preservation: a pure plan may legally retain columns or defer a filter.
The research architecture separates denotation-preserving transformations,
decidable evidence for their premises, and policy selection. The P/C rules below
provide concrete restricted instances, rather than a complete calculus of that
larger design space.

\subsection{Dependencies and graph extraction}
\label{sec:graph-extraction}
For clause $c_i$, D-analysis derives
$\Sigma;B;T\vdash c_i\triangleright(R_i,O_i,m_i,a_i,K_i)$:
required/provided names, movement bit, optional atom arity, and literal positions.
$B$ is the written prefix; $T\subseteq B$ contains preceding let bindings.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{D-Guard}{R=\uses(e,B)}
 {\Sigma;B;T\vdash\mathsf{if}\ e\triangleright(R,\emptyset,m_e,\bot,\emptyset)}

\Rule{D-Bind}{N=\set(\binds(p))\quad R=\uses(e,B)}
 {\Sigma;B;T\vdash\mathsf{let}\ p[:\tau]=e\triangleright(R,N,m_{p,e},\bot,\emptyset)}

\Rule{D-Atom}{\operatorname{walk}(\bar a,B)=(L,R,K,m)}
 {\Sigma;B;T\vdash R_0(\bar a)\triangleright(R\cup(L\cap T),L,m,|\bar a|,K)}
\end{mathpar}
The left-to-right argument walk starts with empty $L,R,K$: a bare name enters
$L$; a literal position enters $K$; a wildcard contributes nothing; an expression
$e$ adds $\uses(e,B\cup L)\setminus L$ to $R$ and prevents movement.
After each clause, extend $B$ by $O_i$ and, for let, $T$ by $O_i$.
Thus atom providers of a shared name are interchangeable; a preceding let is
an obligatory producer.

Define $M(c)$ to hold for: an atom with only bare/wildcard/literal arguments;
a single-identifier let with literal RHS, no reference or subpattern modifier;
or a recognized variable/integer comparison whose variables have syntactically
primitive i32 type at every occurrence/binding. Type accumulation precedes
guard classification; aliases are not resolved. Set $m_i=M(c_i)$.
This whitelist is sufficient movement evidence for the supplied planner. Failure
of $M$ is not a semantic proof that a pure expression cannot move; stronger
host-law evidence can justify additional placements in another planner.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{D-Move}{M(c)}{\Sigma\vdash c:\mathsf{movable}}

\Rule{D-Barrier}{\neg M(c)}{\Sigma\vdash c:\mathsf{barrier}}

\Rule{D-Graph}{\operatorname{distinctBareHead}(q)\quad\operatorname{headBound}(q)\\
 \forall c\in q.\ c\text{ is a distinct-bare atom or primitive guard}}
 {\Sigma\vdash q:\mathsf{graphDomain}}
\end{mathpar}
D-Graph allows cyclic/disconnected queries. Other supported queries use the
prefix algorithm as a whole; negation/aggregation have no implemented D-rule.

Number positive occurrences $A_i$ and guards $p_j$ separately in source order;
prefix commands instead use full-body positions. CQ well-formedness requires
$n\ge1$ positive occurrences. Write $O=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, $X_i=\vars(A_i)$,
and normalize raw-identifier spelling. For declared attributes $a_{ij}$:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{H-Occurrence}{A_i=R_i(\bar x_i)\quad\Sigma(R_i)=(\bar a_i:\bar\tau_i)\\
 m_i=\{a_{ij}\mapsto x_{ij}\}_j\quad X_i=\{x_{ij}\}_j}
 {\Sigma\vdash A_i\leadsto(i,R_i,m_i,X_i)}

\Rule{H-Incidence}{V_q=\bigcup_{i\in O}X_i\quad\mathcal I=\{(i,x)\mid i\in O,\ x\in X_i\}}
 {\bar A\leadsto\mathcal H_q=(V_q,O,\mathcal I)}

\Rule{H-Adjacency}{i<j\quad S_{ij}=X_i\cap X_j\ne\emptyset}
 {\mathcal H_q\vdash i\xleftrightarrow{S_{ij}}j\in G_q}

\Rule{H-JoinTree}{J\text{ is a rooted tree on }O\quad
 \forall x\in V_q.\ J[\{i\mid x\in X_i\}]\text{ is connected}}
 {\mathcal H_q\vdash J:\mathsf{joinTree}}
\end{mathpar}
$\mathcal I$ is also the bipartite occurrence/variable graph; $G_q$ is its SIP
view (AHV \S6.1), with no guard vertices. Occurrence labels preserve repeated
and empty edges; identifying duplicates and removing empties recovers AHV's
hypergraph (\S6.4). H-JoinTree characterizes alpha-acyclicity, using whole
separators $X_i\cap X_j$, possibly empty between components. An unlabeled
adjacency triangle can represent either a star or a cyclic query.

\subsection{GYO recognition}
\label{sec:gyo-construction}
State $(Z,\pi)$ holds residual occurrence sets and an absorption parent map,
initially $Z(i)=X_i$, $\pi(i)=\bot$. Original incidence is immutable.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{G-Private}{i\in\dom Z\quad x\in Z(i)\quad
 \forall j\in\dom Z\setminus\{i\}.\ x\notin Z(j)}
 {(Z,\pi)\step{G-Private}(Z[i\mapsto Z(i)\setminus\{x\}],\pi)}

\Rule{G-Absorb}{i,j\in\dom Z\quad i\ne j\quad Z(i)\subseteq Z(j)}
 {(Z,\pi)\step{G-Absorb}(Z\setminus\{i\},\pi[i\mapsto j])}

\Rule{G-Accept}{\operatorname{terminal}(Z)\quad Z=\{i\mapsto\emptyset\}}
 {(Z,\pi)\Downarrow_{\rm GYO}\mathsf{Some}(\pi)}

\Rule{G-Reject}{\operatorname{terminal}(Z)\quad Z\ne\{i\mapsto\emptyset\}\text{ for every }i}
 {(Z,\pi)\Downarrow_{\rm GYO}\mathsf{None}}
\end{mathpar}
Terminal means neither transition applies. Algorithm~\ref{alg:gyo} implements
\code{hypergraph::analyze}; H-JoinTree checks its result on original edges.
\begin{algorithm}[tbp]
\caption{GYO recognition: $X\mapsto(\alpha,J)$}
\label{alg:gyo}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{GYO}{$X$}
 \State $S\gets([i\mapsto X_i],[i\mapsto\bot]);\quad\alpha\gets\emptyseq$
 \Loop
  \State $P\gets\{(i,x)\mid\mathsf{Private}(i,x)\text{ applies at }S\}$
  \State $A\gets\{(i,j)\mid\mathsf{Absorb}(i,j)\text{ applies at }S\}$
  \State \textbf{if} $P=A=\emptyset$ \textbf{then return} $(\alpha,\operatorname{finish}_G(S))$
  \State $\rho\gets\mathsf{Private}(\min P)$ if $P\ne\emptyset$, else $\mathsf{Absorb}(\min A)$
  \State \Call{Apply}{$S,\alpha,\rho$}
 \EndLoop
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
Here $\operatorname{finish}_G$ is G-Accept/G-Reject; minima are lexicographic.
\begin{lemma}[Ear correspondence]
On distinct nonempty original edges, each absorption is an AHV ear removal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
While two original edges containing $x$ remain active, neither residual can
first delete $x$ by Private. Hence $X_i\cap X_j=Z(i)\cap Z(j)$ for active
$i,j$. Absorbing $i$ into $j$ therefore satisfies the ear premise
$X_i\cap\bigcup_{k\in\dom Z\setminus\{i\}}X_k\subseteq X_j$. Duplicate/empty-edge deletions
stutter after passage to the textbook hypergraph.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[GYO termination and recognition]
Every derivation terminates; it succeeds iff $X$ admits a join tree, and its
parent map is such a tree for the original occurrences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$|\dom Z|+\sum_i|Z(i)|$ strictly decreases. Private preserves join-tree
existence because the removed variable occurs in one active edge. Absorption
also preserves it: restore a contained edge as a leaf of its parent; to delete
it, attach each separated component to the containing parent at its former
neighbor. Every variable crossing these cuts belongs to that parent, preserving
running intersection. In a join tree, a leaf has either a private variable or
is contained in its neighbor; a singleton nonempty edge has a private variable.
Thus an acyclic terminal state is one empty edge. Reversing the reductions
reconstructs a join tree, with each recorded child attached to its recorded
parent. This proves both directions, including duplicate/empty edges.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Assembly and retention}
\label{sec:join-order}\label{sec:retention}
An ordered binary assembly tree $T::=i\mid(T\bowtie T)$ records occurrence
coverage independently of the join tree:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{O-Leaf}{i\in O}{i:\{i\}}

\Rule{O-Join}{T_1:C\quad T_2:E\quad C\cap E=\emptyset}
 {(T_1\bowtie T_2):C\cup E}.
\end{mathpar}
Write $\operatorname{occ}(T)=C$ when $T:C$. Frontier $F(g)=(C,V,r)$ records
coverage, sort, and result; coverages partition introduced occurrences.
A ghost forest starts with $\mathcal T(i)=i$; Combine replaces its two trees
by their join, other rules preserve them, and Output covers $O$.
Interpreting leaves by $L_i$ and joins recursively gives
$\sem{\mathcal E_L(T)}=\bowtie_{i\in\operatorname{occ}(T)}L_i$ by induction.
The executable DAG also retains unary and semijoin-support dependencies.

Let $H$ be the head's set of names, $P$ pending guards,
$X(C)=\bigcup_{i\in C}X_i$, and $\bar C=O\setminus C$. Retention requires
\[
 K(C,P)=X(C)\cap(H\cup X(\bar C)\cup\vars(P))\subseteq V_C\subseteq X(C).
\]
Consequently $V_C\cap V_E=X(C)\cap X(E)=S_X(C,E)$ for disjoint groups:
each shared variable is an outside use on both sides. This is the exact emitted
join key after pruning. Combine contracts these vertices of the quotient
adjacency graph. Empty keys give Cartesian product; $V_E\subseteq V_C$ gives
membership; otherwise the join supplies $V_E\setminus V_C$ from the same matching tuple.
After introduction, the global invariant is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:global}
 \operatorname{out}_{\bar h}\!\left(\pi_H\sigma_{\bigwedge P}
   \left(\mathop{\bowtie}_{(C,V,r)\in F}\sem r\right)\right)=\sem q_I.
\end{equation}
Applied guards are already reflected in the frontier relations.
Initially $D,F$ are empty, $P$ contains all guards, $i=0$, and $\phi=\mathsf{Starting}$.
Emission appends a fresh named result, checking the graph route's cap first.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{P-Start}{\phi=\mathsf{Starting}\quad i<n\quad
 \emit(D,\delta_{m_i}(R_i))=(D',r_i)}
 {(D,F,P,i,\phi)\step{P-Start}
 (D',F[i\mapsto(\{i\},X_i,r_i)],P,i+1,\phi')}

\Rule{P-Select}{\phi\in\{\mathsf{Local},\mathsf{Assembly}\}\quad
 p\in P\quad F(g)=(C,V,r)\quad\vars(p)\subseteq V\\
 \emit(D,\sigma_pr)=(D',s)}
 {(D,F,P,\phi)\step{P-Select}(D',F[g\mapsto(C,V,s)],P\setminus\{p\},\phi)}

\Rule{P-Begin}{\phi=\mathsf{Local}\quad i=n\quad\nexists\text{ ready Select}}
 {(D,F,P,\phi)\step{P-Begin}(D,F,P,\phi_J)}

\Rule{P-Combine}{F(g)=(C,V,r)\quad F(h)=(E,W,s)\quad C\cap E=\emptyset\\
 \phi=\mathsf{Assembly}\quad\text{no ready Select/Trim}\quad\emit(D,r\bowtie s)=(D',t)}
 {(D,F,P,\phi)\step{P-Combine}
 (D',(F\setminus\{g,h\})[g\mapsto(C\cup E,V\cup W,t)],P,\phi)}

\Rule{P-Trim}{F(g)=(C,V,r)\quad U=K(C,P)\subsetneq V\quad
 \phi=\mathsf{Assembly}\\\text{no ready Select}\quad\emit(D,\pi_Ur)=(D',s)}
 {(D,F,P,\phi)\step{P-Trim}(D',F[g\mapsto(C,U,s)],P,\phi)}

\Rule{P-Output}{\phi=\mathsf{Assembly}\quad F=\{g\mapsto(O,H,r)\}\quad P=\emptyset}
 {(D,F,P,\phi)\step{P-Output}(D,\mathsf{output}\ r\ \mathsf{as}\ h(\bar h))}
\end{mathpar}
$\phi'=\mathsf{Local}$ iff $i+1=n$; otherwise Starting. The completed counter
is implicit thereafter. $\phi_J$ is Reduction with a checked tree, Assembly
otherwise. Begin initializes reduction without pruning. Trim uses exact $K$;
retaining any intermediate superset is semantically safe but fails this eager policy.
\begin{lemma}[Retention]
If $V\cap(W\cup H\cup\vars(P))\subseteq U\subseteq V$, then
$\pi_H\sigma_P(L\bowtie M)=\pi_H\sigma_P(\pi_UL\bowtie M)$
for $L:V$, $M:W$ and well-scoped head/predicates.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Restrict a left witness for one inclusion. For the converse choose its original
witness in $L$: every coordinate shared with $M$ or observed by head/predicates
is retained, so the witness remains compatible and gives the same output.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Assembly preservation and termination]
Under $\mathcal H_{\rm rel}$, every complete graph derivation denotes $q$;
the specified policy terminates or rejects its arity cap.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Rename establishes the occurrence valuations. Select distributes a conjunct
into a factor containing its variables; Combine uses join associativity; Trim
uses retention; all preserve Eq.~\eqref{eq:global}. Complete coverage and no
pending guards establish Output. The assembly measure
$(|F|,|P|,\sum_g|V_g|)$ decreases lexicographically. Introduction is finite;
reduction is justified next.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Yannakakis reduction}
\label{sec:yannakakis}
For occurrence relations $L_i:X_i$, global consistency means
$L_i=\pi_{X_i}(\bowtie_jL_j)$ for every $i$; pairwise consistency only requires
$L_i\ltimes L_j=L_i$ for every $i,j$. A full reducer establishes global
consistency on every instance (AHV \S6.4).
Fix a checked tree $J$, root $o$, parent $\operatorname{pa}$, children
$\operatorname{ch}$, nonroot nodes $E_J$, subtrees $T_i$, and separators
$S_c=X_c\cap X_{\operatorname{pa}(c)}$. Running intersection gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:separator-cut}
 X(T_c)\cap X(O\setminus T_c)=S_c.
\end{equation}
A variable crossing the cut must occur at both endpoints. Thus
$a\ltimes_{S_c}b=\{t\in a\mid t\restrict S_c\in\pi_{S_c}b\}$ is the existing
RA semijoin. For empty separators it tests nonemptiness.

After local selections, fix $F_0(i)=(\{i\},X_i,r_i^0)$, $L_i=\sem{r_i^0}$,
and pending $P_0$. The reduction context is
$\mathcal Y(D,\mathbf r,U,Dn)=(D,F_0,P_0,\mathsf{Reduction};J,\mathbf r,U,Dn)$:
latest result references and completed upward/downward child identities.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{Y-Init}{F_0(i)=(\{i\},X_i,r_i^0)\ (i<n)\quad J\text{ checked}\quad\nexists\text{ ready Select}}
 {(D,F_0,P_0,\mathsf{Local};\bot)\step{P-Begin}
 \mathcal Y(D,(r_i^0)_i,\emptyset,\emptyset)}

\Rule{Y-Up}{c\in E_J\setminus U\quad\operatorname{ch}(c)\subseteq U\quad Dn=\emptyset\\
 p=\operatorname{pa}(c)\quad\emit(D,\mathbf r(p)\ltimes_{S_c}\mathbf r(c))=(D',s)}
 {\mathcal Y(D,\mathbf r,U,Dn)\step{Y-Up}\mathcal Y(D',\mathbf r[p\mapsto s],U\cup\{c\},Dn)}

\Rule{Y-Down}{U=E_J\quad c\in E_J\setminus Dn\quad p=\operatorname{pa}(c)\quad(p=o\lor p\in Dn)\\
 \emit(D,\mathbf r(c)\ltimes_{S_c}\mathbf r(p))=(D',s)}
 {\mathcal Y(D,\mathbf r,U,Dn)\step{Y-Down}\mathcal Y(D',\mathbf r[c\mapsto s],U,Dn\cup\{c\})}

\Rule{Y-Freeze}{U=Dn=E_J\quad F'(i)=(\{i\},X_i,\mathbf r(i))\ (i<n)}
 {\mathcal Y(D,\mathbf r,U,Dn)\step{Y-Freeze}(D,F',P_0,\mathsf{Assembly};\bot)}
\end{mathpar}
Y-Init is Begin's tree branch, not another trace event. No sort changes during
reduction: each step reads both latest versions and replaces only its target
reference; Freeze transfers references without emission.
\begin{algorithm}[tbp]
\caption{Yannakakis sweeps after Y-Init}
\label{alg:ya}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Procedure{Reduce}{$S,\delta,J$}
 \State $B\gets[\operatorname{root}(J)];\quad k\gets0$
 \While{$k<|B|$}
  \State Append $\operatorname{children}(J,B_k)$ in increasing ID order to $B$; $k\gets k+1$
 \EndWhile
 \State \textbf{for} $c$ in $[B_{n-1},\ldots,B_1]$ \textbf{do} \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Up}(c)$}
 \State \textbf{for} $c$ in $[B_1,\ldots,B_{n-1}]$ \textbf{do} \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Down}(c)$}
 \State \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Freeze}$}
\EndProcedure
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
For $Q=\bowtie_iL_i$, the upward invariant and its completed form are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:up-support}
 \sem{\mathbf r(i)}=\pi_{X_i}\!\left(\bowtie_{j\in\Omega_i(U)}L_j\right),\quad
 \Omega_i(U)=\{i\}\cup\bigcup_{c\in\operatorname{ch}(i)\cap U}T_c,\quad
 A_i=\pi_{X_i}\!\left(\bowtie_{j\in T_i}L_j\right).
\end{equation}
The cut identity gives, with $B_c=\pi_{S_c}(\bowtie_{j\notin T_c}L_j)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:outside-support}
 \pi_{X_c}Q=A_c\ltimes_{S_c}B_c.
\end{equation}
During Down, $\{o\}\cup Dn$ is ancestor closed and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:down-support}
 \sem{\mathbf r(i)}=\begin{cases}\pi_{X_i}Q,&i\in\{o\}\cup Dn,\\A_i,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\begin{theorem}[Yannakakis full reduction]
The sweeps preserve $Q$ and emit $2(n-1)$ semijoins unless the cap rejects.
At Freeze, $\sem{\mathbf r(i)}=\pi_{X_i}Q$ for every occurrence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A full-join witness supplies support for every semijoin, so each replacement
preserves $Q$. In Up, completed children supply $A_c$. Their subtrees intersect
the accumulated target only through $S_c$; joining their witnesses proves
Eq.~\eqref{eq:up-support}. On completion the root equals $\pi_{X_o}Q$.
For Down to $c$, choose a subtree witness for a surviving tuple in $A_c$ and
a full-join witness for its matching, globally supported parent tuple.
Restrict the latter to the complement of $T_c$; Eq.~\eqref{eq:separator-cut}
allows their union. Conversely each full witness survives. This proves
Eq.~\eqref{eq:down-support}. Reverse/forward BFS supplies the required completed
children/parent respectively, visiting each nonroot edge once per direction.
Empty separators and the one-node case satisfy the same argument.
\end{proof}
This reducer includes applied local guards, not pending cross-occurrence
predicates: these still require assembly. \code{up}, \code{down}, and
\code{freeze} in \code{rules.rs} implement the transitions; the independent
checker validates directions, latest operands, unchanged sorts, and completion.

\subsection{Policy, width, and the compilation judgment}
\label{sec:planning-policy}\label{sec:join-arity}
Let $G=\dom F$, $|G|>1$. Pair selection emits no RA; minima below are lexicographic.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{S-Written}{g=\min G\quad h=\min(G\setminus\{g\})}
 {\mathsf{pick}_{\rm Written}(F)=(g,h)}

\Rule{S-Connected}{g=\min G\quad N=\{h\in G\setminus\{g\}\mid V_g\cap V_h\ne\emptyset\}\\
 h=\min(\text{if }N\ne\emptyset\text{ then }N\text{ else }G\setminus\{g\})}
 {\mathsf{pick}_{\rm Connected}(F)=(g,h)}

\Rule{S-Greedy}{(w,g,h)=\min\{(|V_i\cup V_j|,i,j)\mid i,j\in G,\ i<j\}}
 {\mathsf{pick}_{\rm GreedyWidth}(F)=(g,h)}

\Rule{S-Tree}{r=\operatorname{root}(J)\quad
 i=\min\{j\in G\setminus\{r\}\mid\operatorname{pa}(j)\in C_r\}}
 {\mathsf{pick}_{\rm Auto,J}(F)=(r,i)}
\end{mathpar}
Auto without a tree uses GreedyWidth; other policies use their named rule.
Written/Connected grow the least group; GreedyWidth admits bushy assembly.
S-Tree maintains a root-connected coverage and all other groups singleton.
A path to an uncovered node supplies a candidate; running intersection makes
the selected join key its parent separator. This ID-based assembly order is
independent of BFS reduction order.

For $r:V$, let $a(r)=|V|$. Raw join arity, retained arity, and reported peak are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a_{\bowtie}(C,E)&=|V_C\cup V_E|=|V_C|+|V_E|-|S_X(C,E)|,\\
 a_{\rm retained}(C,P)&=|K(C,P)|,\qquad
 w(D)=\max(\{0\}\cup\{a(r)\mid r=o\in D\}).
 \end{aligned}
\]
All named results count, including original Renames, reductions, and joins
before pruning. With optional cap $b$ and sort environment $\Gamma$:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{E-Emit}{\Gamma\vdash o:V\quad r\text{ fresh}\quad(b=\bot\lor |V|\le b)}
 {\Gamma;b\vdash(D,o)\leadsto(D\concat[r=o],\Gamma[r\mapsto V],r)}

\Rule{E-Arity}{\Gamma\vdash o:V\quad b\ne\bot\quad |V|>b}
 {\Gamma;b\vdash(D,o)\leadsto\mathsf{arityError}\quad[D\text{ unchanged}]}
\end{mathpar}
Graph emission checks the cap before mutation; prefix planning checks completed
RA. The checker recomputes $w(D)$. GreedyWidth minimizes only the next raw arity;
a cap rejection does not establish global infeasibility.
For $c_I(r)=|\sem r_I|$, the notes' row-volume proxy is
$W_0(D,I)=\sum_{r=o\in D,\ o\text{ NaturalJoin}}c_I(r)$.
Materialized \code{stats.results} exposes each count; \code{join_pairs} equals
$W_0$ under set assumptions, including membership joins but excluding reduction
and indexing. Bytes and decomposition widths are different measures;
cardinality/WCOJ optimization remains Stage II.

Let \textsc{Eager} repeatedly apply the least ready Select pair $(p,g)$,
otherwise the least applicable Trim group, until neither exists.
\begin{algorithm}[tbp]
\caption{Graph-domain planning for well-formed $q$: $(D,o,\alpha,J,\delta)$}
\label{alg:graph-plan}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{GraphPlan}{$q,s,b$}
 \State $X\gets\operatorname{incidence}(q);\quad(\alpha,J)\gets(\emptyseq,\mathsf{None})$
 \State \textbf{if} $s=\mathsf{Auto}$ \textbf{then} $(\alpha,J)\gets\Call{GYO}{X}$
 \State \textbf{if} $J\ne\mathsf{None}$ \textbf{then} check H-JoinTree on $X$
 \State $S\gets\operatorname{JoinState}(q,J,b);\quad\delta\gets\emptyseq$
 \State \textbf{for} $i=0,\ldots,n-1$ \textbf{do} \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Introduce}(i)$}
 \State \textbf{while} a ready Select $\rho$ exists \textbf{do} \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\rho$}
 \State \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Begin}$}; \textbf{if} $J\ne\mathsf{None}$ \textbf{then} \Call{Reduce}{$S,\delta,J$}
 \State \Call{Eager}{$S,\delta$}
 \While{$|\dom S.F|>1$}
  \State $(g,h)\gets\mathsf{pick}_{s,J}(S.F)$; \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Combine}(g,h)$}
  \State \Call{Eager}{$S,\delta$}
 \EndWhile
 \State \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Output}(\text{sole group})$}; check local RA contract
 \State \Return $(S.D,S.o,\alpha,J,\delta)$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
The initial Select loop uses the same least $(p,g)$ choice. $\mathsf{pick}_{s,J}$
is the dispatcher defined above; \code{strategy.rs} implements these choices.
With successful D-analysis $\mathcal D$ and the algorithms' return conventions:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{Plan-Graph}{\Sigma\vdash q:\mathsf{wfCQ}\quad\Sigma\vdash q:\mathsf{graphDomain}\\
 \mathsf{GraphPlan}(q,s,b)=(D,o,\alpha,J,\delta)}
 {\Sigma\vdash_s^b q\Downarrow(D,o;\alpha,J,\delta,w(D))}

\Rule{Plan-Prefix}{\Sigma\vdash q:\mathsf{wfCQ}\quad\Sigma\not\vdash q:\mathsf{graphDomain}\\
 \mathsf{PrefixPlan}(q,\mathcal D,s,b)=(D,o,\delta)}
 {\Sigma\vdash_s^b q\Downarrow(D,o;\emptyseq,\mathsf{None},\delta,w(D))}
\end{mathpar}
The target retains source annotation and $(D,o)$; other components form the
report. Checking establishes trace legality; \code{compile_cq_derivation}
accepts other legal schedules without enforcing canonical policy choice.

\subsection{Canonical and dependency-constrained prefixes}
\label{sec:prefix-planning}
Canonical CQ lowering uses written occurrence order, full joins, and final pruning only.
For $a_i=\delta_{m_i}(R_i)$, starting at $(\emptyseq,\bot;0)$:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{B-Root}{p=\bot\quad\emit(D,a_i)=(D',r)}
 {(D,p;i)\Rightarrow_B(D',r;i+1)}

\Rule{B-Join}{p\ne\bot\quad\emit(D,a_i)=(D_1,r)\quad\emit(D_1,p\bowtie r)=(D',s)}
 {(D,p;i)\Rightarrow_B(D',s;i+1)}

\Rule{B-Output}{i=n\quad\emit(D,\pi_Hp)=(D',r)}
 {(D,p;i)\Rightarrow_B(D',\mathsf{output}\ r\ \mathsf{as}\ h(\bar h))}
\end{mathpar}
Induction gives its complete join followed by head projection. Extended CQ
uses the following C-* rules, with Written policy for canonical lowering.

For body $c_0,\ldots,c_{m-1}$, D-facts define barrier edges
$E_{\rm barrier}=\{(i,j)\mid i<j\land(\neg m_i\lor\neg m_j)\}$.
Availability is additionally a set condition: a required name can have alternative
atom providers, whereas a computed key has directed inputs. State
$S=(D,p,B,U,K,o)$ holds definitions, optional prefix, complete sort, pending
clauses, normalized literal keys, and optional output. Initially $D,B,K$
are empty, $U$ is the full clause set, and $p,o=\bot$. Fresh-name supply is
implicit. \textit{unitize} emits $\mathbf1$ only when no prefix exists.
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{C-Ready}{\operatorname{normalized}(K)\quad o=\bot\quad i\in U\quad R_i\subseteq B\\
 \forall j\in U.\ j<i\Rightarrow(m_i\land m_j)\quad
 (c_i\text{ is Bind}\Rightarrow O_i\cap B=\emptyset)}
 {S\vdash i:\mathsf{ready}}

\Rule{C-Constant}{(i,j)\text{ next unnormalized literal}\quad U=\{0,\ldots,m-1\}\quad k\text{ fresh}\\
 (D',p')=\operatorname{typedBind}(D,p,k,\tau_{ij},\operatorname{arg}(c_i,j))}
 {(D,p,B,U,K,\bot)\step{C-Constant}(D',p',B\cup\{k\},U,K[(i,j)\mapsto k],\bot)}

\Rule{C-Guard}{S\vdash i:\mathsf{ready}\quad c_i=\mathsf{if}\ e\\
 (D_0,p_0)=\operatorname{unitize}(D,p)\quad\emit(D_0,\sigma_ep_0)=(D',p')}
 {S\step{C-Guard}(D',p',B,U\setminus\{i\},K,\bot)}

\Rule{C-Bind}{S\vdash i:\mathsf{ready}\quad c_i=\mathsf{let}\ p_0[:\tau]=e\\
 N=\binds(p_0)\quad(D',p')=\operatorname{typedBind}(D,p,p_0,\tau,e)}
 {S\step{C-Bind}(D',p',B\cup\set(N),U\setminus\{i\},K,\bot)}
\end{mathpar}
Here $\operatorname{arg}(c_i,j)$ and $\tau_{ij}$ are its actual argument and declared type.
\textit{typedBind} unitizes and emits Extend with
\code{\{ let PAT: TYPE = EXPR; (BOUND_NAMES,) \}}; annotation is optional,
and zero bindings omit the tail expression. No rule follows an existing output.

C-Match walks arguments with context $(D,p,\widehat B,L,M,E)$: prospective
sort, earlier bare names, positional map, and delayed equalities; $B_0$ is the
original prefix sort. Ordered cases define the A-judgment:
\begin{mathpar}

\Rule{A-Constant}{K(i,j)=k}{a_j\leadsto_A k\quad[\text{no emission}]}

\Rule{A-Variable}{a_j=x\quad x\notin L}{a_j\leadsto_A x\quad[L:=L\cup\{x\}]}

\Rule{A-Repeat}{a_j=x\in L\quad k\text{ fresh}}
 {a_j\leadsto_A k\quad[E:=E\concat(k=x)]}

\Rule{A-Wildcard}{a_j=\_\quad k\text{ fresh}}
 {a_j\leadsto_A k\quad[\text{distinct witness}]}

\Rule{A-Prefix}{a_j=e\quad\uses(e,L\setminus B_0)=\emptyset\quad k\text{ fresh}}
 {a_j\leadsto_A k\quad[p:=\operatorname{ext}_{\{k:\tau_j\},(e,)}(\operatorname{unitize}(p))]}

\Rule{A-Local}{a_j=e\quad\uses(e,L\setminus B_0)\ne\emptyset\quad k\text{ fresh}}
 {a_j\leadsto_A k\quad[E:=E\concat(k.\mathsf{eq}(\&e))]}
\end{mathpar}
Each case requires earlier cases inapplicable, appends its schema-position
mapping to $M$, and inserts the result name into $\widehat B$.
A-Prefix extends before matching; A-Local tests after same-atom variables exist.
Only completed clause states require $B=\sort(p)$.
\[
\Rule{C-Match}{S\vdash i:\mathsf{ready}\quad c_i=R(\bar a)\\
 (D,p,B,\emptyset,\emptyset,\emptyseq)\leadsto_A^*(D_1,p_1,B_1,L,M,E)\\
 (D_2,r)=\emit(D_1,\delta_M(R))\quad(D_3,t)=\operatorname{joinPrefix}(D_2,p_1,r)\\
 (D',p')=\operatorname{selectList}(D_3,t,E)}
 {S\step{C-Match}(D',p',B_1,U\setminus\{i\},K,\bot)}.
\]
\textit{joinPrefix} joins an existing prefix or returns $r$;
\textit{selectList} emits equalities in argument order. The trace records Match once.
Let $\operatorname{keepHead}(B,h)$ be bare-head dependencies, or all $B$ for a
computed head. Witnesses survive until Finish:
\[
\Rule{C-Finish}{\operatorname{normalized}(K)\quad U=\emptyset\quad o=\bot\quad W=\operatorname{keepHead}(B,h)\\
 (D_0,p_0)=\operatorname{unitize}(D,p)\quad\emit(D_0,\pi_Wp_0)=(D',r)}
 {S\step{C-Finish}(D',r,W,\emptyset,K,\mathsf{output}\ r\ \mathsf{as}\ h(\bar e))}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Prefix lowering]
Under $\mathcal H_{\rm rel}$ and CQ well-scoping, a complete C-derivation
denotes $q$; its policy terminates or rejects with a diagnostic.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on written clauses. Constant/Repeat enforce positional equalities,
Variable joins shared names, and Wildcard retains a witness. Prefix extension
and Local selection enforce their expression's value after its inputs exist.
Guard restricts and Bind extends this relation; Finish applies the head.
For another legal schedule, move each chosen clause left across earlier pending
clauses. C-Ready requires both sides movable and inputs available: relational
atoms commute, a fresh literal binding has no inputs, and an available primitive
guard commutes with extensions. Under $\mathcal H_{\rm rel}$ these exchanges
preserve denotation. Normalization is finite and every clause removes one pending
identity; the least pending written clause is ready for valid written scope.
\end{proof}

For atoms put $j_i=|O_i\cap B|+|K_i|$ and $e_i=|B|+a_i-j_i$ for
GreedyWidth/Auto, otherwise $e_i=0$. Choose lexicographically by
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_s(i,B)=\begin{cases}
 (0,0,0,i),&s=\mathsf{Written}\text{ or }c_i\text{ is not an atom},\\
 (1,[j_i=0],e_i,i),&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Non-Written policies prefer guards/bindings, then shared/literal keys, then
estimated union arity including anonymous/repeated positional columns.
\begin{algorithm}[tbp]
\caption{Prefix planning with successful D-analysis $\mathcal D$}
\label{alg:prefix}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{PrefixPlan}{$q,\mathcal D,s,b$}
 \State $S\gets\operatorname{ClauseState}(q,\mathcal D);\quad\delta\gets\emptyseq$
 \State \textbf{for} literal $(i,j)$ in lexicographic order \textbf{do} \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Constant}(i,j)$}
 \While{$S.U\ne\emptyset$}
  \State $R\gets\{i\in S.U\mid S\vdash i:\mathsf{ready}\}$; \textbf{reject} if $R=\emptyset$
  \State $i\gets\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{i\in R}\operatorname{rank}_s(i,S.B)$
  \State $\rho\gets\mathsf{Match}(i)$, $\mathsf{Guard}(i)$, or $\mathsf{Bind}(i)$ according to $c_i$
  \State \textbf{reject} unsupported clause; otherwise \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\rho$}
 \EndWhile
 \State \Call{Apply}{$S,\delta,\mathsf{Finish}$}; check local RA contract and $b=\bot\lor w(S.D)\le b$
 \State \Return $(S.D,S.o,\delta)$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

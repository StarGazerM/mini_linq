\section{Back-end IR: recorded representation, decomposition and kernels (design)}
\label{sec:backend-ir}
This section specifies the IR of a new back end, not a verified
implementation. Its rules are \emph{abstract} in the inventory until a pass
and a check implement them. It refines Section~\ref{sec:backend}: $R$ gains
value codes and explicit providers, $E$ keeps strictness, and the control $C$
that an emitter chooses today is split into recorded terms (index spaces,
output protocols, kernels) and genuinely free choices (worker assignment).

\subsection{Recorded choices}
\begin{definition}[Recorded choice]
An operational alternative is admissible at a level $L$ when the alternatives
are distinct $L$ terms whose images under $L$'s abstraction are equal, or
related by $\sqsubseteq$. A checked pass chooses one; its \code{check}
verifies the premises of the law that equates them. A choice that changes no
term at any level is \emph{free}: no level may depend on it.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Emitter]
An emitter maps each recorded construct to one fixed template and rejects a
program containing a construct, or an absent decision, it has no template
for. It performs no analysis whose result changes the emitted program other
than reading recorded entries and their checked facts.
\end{definition}
The current GPU printer does not satisfy the emitter definition. It decides,
without a recorded term: existence instead of enumeration for fully bound
probes; the value codec of every built table (default \code{Plain}, i.e.\
\code{i64}); the index implementation behind \code{SortedRun}; which probe
keys an index answers exactly and which it re-checks; replacing carried values
by row identities; kernel boundaries and the resumable state machine; batch
sizes not written in Structures; and the output reservation protocol. Each of
these is assigned below to the level whose abstraction equates its
alternatives. The CPU pull printer is close to an emitter because sequential
pull control has a single realization and Rust trait dispatch realizes the
recorded provider types.

\subsection{Values: codes}
$R$ abstracts a representation to a bag of rows. A \emph{codec} refines the
values inside rows.
\begin{definition}[Codec]
A codec $\kappa$ for a column type $\tau$ has a value domain
$D_\kappa\subseteq\tau$, a code type $C_\kappa$ and maps
$\mathsf{enc}_\kappa:D_\kappa\to C_\kappa$,
$\mathsf{dec}_\kappa:C_\kappa\to\tau$ with
$\mathsf{dec}_\kappa\circ\mathsf{enc}_\kappa=\mathrm{id}_{D_\kappa}$.
On rows, $\alpha_r(\rho)=\mathrm{Bag}(\mathsf{dec})(\alpha^C_r(\rho))$.
\end{definition}
Examples: the identity; widening to \code{i64}; an order-preserving dictionary
of one join domain with dense \code{u32} IDs; frame-of-reference bit packing.
A column's codec is recorded with its representation. An absent entry is the
identity on $\tau$, never a widening.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{CODE-Leibniz}{\mathsf{dec}_\kappa\text{ injective}\quad
 \text{every side of key }K\text{ uses }\kappa}
 {\text{equality, hashing and dedup on }K\text{ may read codes}}

\Rule{CODE-Order}{\mathsf{enc}_\kappa\text{ monotone on }D_\kappa}
 {\text{range, sort and merge on }c\text{ may read codes}}

\Rule{CODE-Dense}{C_\kappa=[0,n)\quad \mathsf{enc}_\kappa\text{ bijective onto }[0,n)}
 {\text{a directory on }c\text{ may be an array indexed by code}}

\Rule{CODE-Literal}{c:\kappa\quad \ell\in D_\kappa}
 {\text{an occurrence constant }c=\ell\text{ reads code }\mathsf{enc}_\kappa(\ell)}

\Rule{CODE-Pack}{\kappa_1,\ldots,\kappa_m\text{ codes of widths }w_i\quad
 \textstyle\sum_iw_i\le 64}
 {\mathsf{pack}(x_1,\ldots,x_m)=\textstyle\sum_i x_i\,2^{\sum_{j>i}w_j}
 \text{ is an injective, lexicographic row code}}
\end{mathpar}
Join columns share a codec because they share a domain. A value is decoded
only where it leaves the coded fragment: an answer column, a Rust free
variable, or a different codec. A literal outside $D_\kappa$ matches no row.

\subsection{Providers: recorded index implementations}
A provider type names both what an index answers and how. The laws are those
of Section~\ref{sec:structures}; the new types differ only in realization.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PROV-Csr}{\text{rows stably sorted by key code }k\quad
 o[k]=|\{i\mid \mathsf{key}_i<k\}|\quad \text{CODE-Dense on }k}
 {\mathsf{lookup}(k)=[o[k],o[k+1])\text{ is the exact bucket of }k}

\Rule{PROV-Exists}{\mathsf{reads}(b)\subseteq\{\mathsf{probe\ first},\mathsf{semi}\}\quad
 \mathsf{heading}(b)\subseteq K\quad \text{canon downstream}}
 {\text{a membership test realizes every read of }b}

\Rule{PROV-Canon}{\mathsf{canon}\ r\quad
 \text{provider }\in\{\mathsf{hash},\ \mathsf{sort}+\mathsf{unique}\text{ on a CODE-Pack key}\}}
 {\alpha(\mathsf{canon}\ r)=\mathrm{supp}(\alpha(r))}
\end{mathpar}
PROV-Exists is STR-Existential recorded by a pass: it replaces the printer's
silent \code{boolean\_probe}. PROV-Csr is the provider of the hand-written H05
reference: an RCCL radix sort over the key's code bits, then
\code{lower\_bound} of every code. A provider may still choose its internal
algorithm (for example radix or merge sort) when that choice changes no
recorded term.

\subsection{Index spaces: decomposition of a stream}
A total segment of a pipe (ST-Total) evaluated over its producer's positions
is a map over a finite ordered index set. The Space level makes that set a
term.
\begin{lstlisting}
Space ::= rows r                      // positions of a for producer
        | flatten S by b [K]          // ST-Expand's virtual array
        | tile S n                    // consecutive groups of n positions
Entry ::= r space = S [retain]        // the space of r's total segment
        | r output = Protocol;
\end{lstlisting}
$|\mathsf{rows}\ r|=[0,|r|)$. For $\mathsf{flatten}\ S\ \mathsf{by}\ b[K]$, a
position is $(p,j)$ with $p\in|S|$ and $j<|b[K(p)]|$, ordered
lexicographically, with the exclusive offsets of the segmented-flatten lemma.
$\mathsf{tile}\ S\ n$ groups the positions of $S$ into consecutive blocks of
$n$. A segment $f$ over $S$ denotes
\[
 \sem{f\ \mathsf{over}\ S}=\biguplus_{p\in|S|}f(p),
\]
and is $\bot$ if some $f(p)=\bot$, exactly as the bind of
Section~\ref{sec:denotation}.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{SPACE-Flatten}{\mathsf{rangeCursor}(b,K)\quad l\text{ total over }S}
 {\sem{\mathsf{probe}[K](l,b)}
  =\sem{(p,j)\mapsto l(p)\bowtie b[K](p)_j\ \mathsf{over}\ \mathsf{flatten}\ S\ \mathsf{by}\ b[K]}}

\Rule{SPACE-Partition}{\{P_k\}\text{ disjoint}\quad\bigcup_kP_k=|S|\quad
 \text{exhaustive evaluation}}
 {\biguplus_{p\in|S|}f(p)=\biguplus_k\biguplus_{p\in P_k}f(p)}

\Rule{SPACE-Retain}{S\text{ total}\quad
 d=\{(p_0,|b[K(p)]|,\bar v_p)\}_{p}\quad o=\mathsf{exclusiveScan}(|\cdot|)}
 {\mathsf{flatten}\ S\ \mathsf{by}\ b[K]\text{ may be stored as }(d,o)
  \text{ and re-entered by owner search}}

\Rule{SPACE-KeyPartition}{h:\mathit{Row}\to[0,n)\quad
 B_k=\{\!\{t\in B\mid h(t)=k\}\!\}}
 {\mathsf{canon}(B)=\biguplus_k\mathsf{canon}(B_k)}
\end{mathpar}
SPACE-Partition holds for partial $f$ as well: $\bot$ is undistinguished and
aborts the whole evaluation, so which part meets it first is unobservable. It
does not license early stop, and it is stated on bags: parts are disjoint and
covering, because before \code{canon} a lost or duplicated position changes
the bag. Batching across a partial step remains governed by ST-Stage;
$\mathsf{batch}\ N$ (strictness) and a partition (decomposition) are
independent decisions. SPACE-Retain stores a level of a flatten as descriptors
(first row, length, carried values) with exclusive offsets, so a later space
can span all of its positions at once; the owner of a position is found by
binary search over the offsets. Per-part \code{canon} is a sound prefilter,
since $\mathrm{supp}(\biguplus_kB_k)=\bigcup_k\mathrm{supp}(B_k)$, but replaces
the global boundary only under SPACE-KeyPartition.

\paragraph{Free choices.} The order in which parts run, the assignment of
parts to workers, and dynamic tile claiming (a shared cursor) are free: by
SPACE-Partition no term depends on them. The space, its tile sizes per level
and every retained level are recorded, because they change emitted code.

\subsection{Output protocols}
Writing $\biguplus_kB_k$ into one buffer has these realizations, equal under
$\alpha$:
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{OUT-Count}{f\text{ pure}\quad c_k=|B_k|\quad o_k=\textstyle\sum_{j<k}c_j}
 {\text{evaluate }f\text{ twice; part }k\text{ writes at }[o_k,o_k+c_k)}

\Rule{OUT-Reserve}{\mathsf{fetchAdd}\text{ returns disjoint ranges}}
 {\text{part }k\text{ writes }B_k\text{ at its reserved range}}

\Rule{OUT-Block}{\text{tile }T\quad o_i=\mathsf{exclusiveScan}_{i\in T}(c_i)\quad
 \mathit{base}=\mathsf{fetchAdd}(\textstyle\sum_{i\in T}c_i)}
 {\text{lane }i\text{ writes its }c_i\text{ rows at }\mathit{base}+o_i}

\Rule{OUT-Bound}{\text{capacity }m\quad\text{every count, offset and sum is checked}}
 {\text{overflow aborts or retries; it never writes a truncated answer}}
\end{mathpar}
Purity (premise~3 of Section~\ref{sec:premises}) licenses evaluating $f$ more
than once (OUT-Count, a retry after overflow). OUT-Block composes OUT-Count
inside a tile with OUT-Reserve across tiles. Resource exhaustion is outside
the meaning, but a wrapped counter would change the answer, so every bounded
counter is checked.

\subsection{Kernels: the GPU's diverged level}
Only the GPU refines a Space program into kernels; the CPU consumes the Space
level directly (one part per task, or one part for the sequential emitter).
\begin{lstlisting}
Kernel ::= kernel k over S lanes n [shared m] { Step* } ;
Step   ::= load r | probe r [K] | test r [K] | scan c | ballot c
         | reduce c | owner o | emit Protocol
\end{lstlisting}
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{KER-Boundary}{k_1\text{ writes }x\quad k_2\text{ reads }x\\
 x\text{ is a retained Physical result, a retained space or an output}}
 {\text{a kernel boundary at }x\text{ is admissible}}

\Rule{KER-Lane}{\text{lane }l\text{ owns positions }P_l\quad
 \{P_l\}\text{ disjoint and covering}}
 {\text{lane }l\text{ evaluates }P_l\text{ in order with scalar meaning}}

\Rule{KER-Collective}{\text{an exclusive scan, a monoid reduction, a ballot
 or an owner search over lanes}}
 {\text{its result is the sequential fold over the participating lanes}}
\end{mathpar}
A kernel boundary is a materialization, so it sits only where a value is
already retained; it cannot split a pipe without a recorded retention. Lanes
communicate only through collectives, and each collective realizes a law
above: an exclusive scan gives segmented-flatten and OUT-Block offsets, a
reduction gives counts, a ballot gives the survivors of a total filter, an
owner search inverts SPACE-Retain offsets. Per-lane state (registers, shared
memory, spilled continuations) has no meaning, but must respect ST-Stage's
boundaries before partial steps.

\subsection{The H05 reference as recorded terms}
The hand-written reference is expressible in these terms; nothing it does is
outside them.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.45\linewidth}p{0.48\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Reference & Recorded term\\
\midrule
\code{u32} domain IDs, FOR-packed columns & codecs (CODE-Leibniz, CODE-Order, CODE-Dense)\\
radix sort by key bits, \code{lower\_bound} offsets & PROV-Csr on each build\\
Supplier check binds no column & PROV-Exists\\
\code{segments}: Region, Nation, Customer ranges & retained space (SPACE-Retain) over two flattens\\
\code{expand}: 256-position driver tiles over the retained Customer level & $\mathsf{tile}\ 256$ of that space, dynamic claiming free\\
Orders and Lineitem in 1024-position sub-tiles & $\mathsf{tile}\ 1024$ of each nested flatten\\
block scan, one \code{fetch\_add} per block & OUT-Block\\
overflow flag & OUT-Bound\\
packed answer key, radix sort, unique & CODE-Pack and PROV-Canon (sort+unique)\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The compiled plan R records the same plan and builds; it differs only in
decisions the printer currently makes for itself. With every row above
recorded by a checked pass, the two programs have the same recorded terms,
and the remaining difference is emitter templates.

\subsection{Composition and divergence}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Level & Meaning & Recorded choices & Users\\
\midrule
$R$ with codes & $\alpha_r$ with $\mathsf{dec}$ & codecs, providers & all\\
$E$ & strictness & $\mathsf{batch}\ N$, \code{expand} & all\\
Space & $\biguplus$ over positions & spaces, tiles, retained levels, protocol & CPU parallel, GPU\\
Kernels & lanes and collectives & boundaries, lanes, state & GPU\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The first three rows extend shared levels with new terms and keep their
denotation; the kernel level is a refinement that diverges below Space. The
sequential CPU emitter is the one-part instance of Space. The parallel
Datalog extension's partitioned leading scan and grain are a Space policy
over \code{rows}, and its sharded admission is SPACE-KeyPartition. A printer
of either back end realizes these terms and decides none of them.

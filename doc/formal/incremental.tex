\section{Incremental evaluation and Datalog (design)}
\label{sec:incremental}
This section is a design. No rule in it is implemented. It fixes the
semantics that a Datalog level above CQ, maintained structures and delta
evaluation must refine, and assigns every law to exactly one level.
Annotations are restricted to $K\in\{\mathbb B,\mathbb N\}$, time to rounds
$\mathbb N$, and updates to insertions. Z-sets and partially ordered time are
out of scope; \S\ref{sec:incremental-scope} states the extension point.

\subsection{Annotated relations}
\begin{definition}
For a commutative semiring $K$, $\mathrm{Rel}_K(\tau)=\tau\to_{fin}K$. It
carries the free $K$-semimodule monad $M_K$: $\eta(x)=\{x\mapsto1\}$, and bind
multiplies along a valuation and adds across valuations. A semiring
homomorphism $h:K\to K'$ induces a monad morphism $M_K\to M_{K'}$. The support
$\mathrm{supp}:\mathbb N\to\mathbb B$ is the instance used here; it is
idempotent and commutes with bind and $\uplus$.
\end{definition}
The core denotation of \S\ref{sec:composition} is $\bot+M_{\mathbb B}$. A body
atom is a bind, a guard is $\mathsf{guard}$, the head is $\mathsf{fmap}$.
$M_{\mathbb B}$ and $M_{\mathbb N}$ are commutative, so atom order is
denotationally irrelevant. Guard placement is not a commutativity question:
moving a partial guard changes the set of valuations it is applied to and so
whether $\bot$ is reached; total guards move freely.

\subsection{Operational streams and dedup motion}
An operational run of a call yields a bag stream $\sem{q}_{op}$; pull printers
realize it as an unfold, push printers as a fold.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{INC-Adequacy}{q\text{ consumed to exhaustion}}
 {\mathrm{supp}\circ\sem{q}_{op}=\sem{q}}

\Rule{INC-Supp}{e\text{ a bind, }\uplus\text{ or projection context}}
 {\mathrm{supp}(e[\,b\,])=\mathrm{supp}(e[\,\mathrm{supp}(b)\,])}
\end{mathpar}
Every dedup point (\code{canon}, \code{head}, aggregate set folds, Datalog
insertion) is one application of $\mathrm{supp}$; \textsc{INC-Supp} is the only
law that moves it. A consumer that applies $\mathrm{supp}$ itself may receive a
bag; this is the bag-output contract. A fold into a monoid $(M,\cdot)$ needs
$\mathrm{supp}$ first unless $M$ is idempotent (min, max), in which case
folding the bag gives the same value.

\subsection{Representations}
A structure type $S$ has an abstraction $\alpha_S:S\to\mathrm{Rel}_K$; a layout
$L$ has $\mathrm{rep}_L$ with $\alpha\circ\mathrm{rep}_L=\mathrm{id}$.
\begin{obligation}[per type, premise like \textsc{H-Eval}]
Probe: $\alpha(\mathsf{lookup}(s,k))=\sigma_{key=k}\,\alpha(s)$.
Merge: $\alpha(s\oplus s')=\alpha(s)+\alpha(s')$.
Disjoint merge: if $\alpha(s)\cdot\alpha(s')=0$ then $s\oplus s'$ may be the
cheaper $s\uplus s'$. Retyping replaces $S$ and preserves $\alpha$.
\end{obligation}
\begin{definition}
$\mathsf{Runs}\langle S\rangle$ is a sequence of runs $r_i:S$ with round
intervals $[l_i,u_i)$, contiguous and increasing. Its abstraction is
$\sum_i\alpha(r_i)$; $\mathsf{cut}_u=\sum_{u_i\le u}\alpha(r_i)$.
\end{definition}
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{STR-Maintain}{\text{merge obligation for }S\quad
 \text{every required cut }u\text{ is a run boundary}}
 {\alpha(\mathsf{insert}(x,r))=\alpha(x)+\alpha(r)\quad
  \alpha(\mathsf{merge}_i(x))=\alpha(x)\quad
  \mathsf{cut}_u\text{ preserved}}
\end{mathpar}
\begin{theorem}
For every $S$ satisfying the merge obligation, a maintained entry over a
growing host $R$ satisfies $\alpha(x_t)=R^t$ at every call $t$, and every
required cut $u\le t$ equals $R^u$. The proof is by induction on rounds and is
parametric in $S$.
\end{theorem}
Time, run intervals and merge scheduling therefore belong to
$\mathsf{Runs}$, not to $S$; $S$ owes only the merge law. The Structures IR
records which entries are maintained and which cuts readers require; it may
compact only beyond them.

\subsection{Streams of versions}
A relation over rounds is $\mathbb N\to\mathrm{Rel}_K$ with integration $I$,
differentiation $D$ and delay $z^{-1}$; $DI=ID=\mathrm{id}$.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{CQ-Diff}{q=A_1\bowtie\cdots\bowtie A_k\text{ over changing }A_i\quad
 \text{other inputs constant}}
 {D\Big(\prod_j IA_j\Big)=\sum_i\Big(\prod_{j<i}IA_j\Big)\,DA_i\,
  \Big(\prod_{j>i}z^{-1}IA_j\Big)}

\Rule{DL-InsertNew}{\text{insertions only}\quad K=\mathbb B}
 {D(\mathrm{supp}\circ I)\,N_t=\mathrm{supp}(N_t)\setminus
  (I\,\mathrm{supp}\,N)_{t-1}}
\end{mathpar}
\textsc{CQ-Diff} is exact in $\mathbb N$. Under $\mathrm{supp}$ any family of
terms covering every valuation with at least one changed atom is sound;
reading the current version at every non-delta position is such a cover and
only enlarges the bag $N_t$. The exact form needs $\mathsf{cut}$ at the previous
round. \textsc{DL-InsertNew} is the only non-linear step; it is an anti-join
against the canonical entry, which must therefore be stateful. By
\textsc{STR-Maintain}, $\mathrm{rep}_L\circ I=I_\oplus\circ\mathrm{rep}_L$.

\subsection{Datalog}
A program has extensional hosts and intensional relations defined by rules
whose bodies are core bodies. Negated atoms and aggregates refer only to lower
strata. Termination requires that heads in a recursive component copy columns
of positive atoms.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{DL-Strata}{G\text{ the dependency graph}\quad
 \text{no negative or aggregate edge inside an SCC of }G}
 {\sem{P}=\mathrm{lfp}\text{ of each SCC in topological order}}

\Rule{DL-Delta}{F_1..F_k\text{ the variants of a rule}\quad
 \text{erasure of delta and cut renames gives the rule}\\
 \text{each changing position read as delta in exactly one }F_i}
 {F(R\cup\Delta)=F(R)\cup\textstyle\bigcup_iF_i(R\cup\Delta,\Delta)}
\end{mathpar}
Within one SCC let $F$ be monotone. Naive evaluation is
$R^{t+1}=F(R^t)$. Semi-naive evaluation is
\[
\Delta^{t+1}=\mathrm{supp}\Big(\biguplus_iF_i(R^t,\Delta^t)\Big)\setminus R^t,
\qquad R^{t+1}=R^t\uplus\Delta^{t+1}.
\]
\begin{theorem}
$R^t_{semi}=R^t_{naive}$ for every $t$. Each valuation is enumerated in the
round in which its last delta atom appears, so a partial guard sees the same
valuation as in naive evaluation and $\bot$ arises exactly when it does there.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Induction on $t$ using \textsc{DL-Delta}, monotonicity
($R^t\subseteq F(R^t)$), and \textsc{DL-InsertNew}.
\end{proof}
Within a round, every variant reads hosts that are immutable for the call:
the answer iterator borrows the current entry, so Rust rejects insertion until
it is dropped. Composing \textsc{INC-Adequacy}, \textsc{STR-Maintain}, the
theorem above and each pass checker gives
$\mathrm{supp}\circ\sem{\text{generated Rust}}=\sem{P}$ under the premises of
\S\ref{sec:incremental-scope}.

\subsection{Ownership of laws}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Level & Concept & Law\\
\midrule
Datalog & strata, fixpoint, insertion & \textsc{DL-Strata}, \textsc{DL-Delta}, \textsc{DL-InsertNew}\\
CQ/RA & variants and the cuts they read & \textsc{CQ-Diff}\\
CQ contract & bag output & \textsc{INC-Adequacy}, \textsc{INC-Supp}\\
Structures IR & maintained entries, required cuts & \textsc{STR-Maintain}\\
Runtime types & $S$, merge, probe & obligations (premise)\\
Rust & isolation within a round & borrow checking\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Choices recorded at their level: variant form (exact or cover), merge policy
(eager $\uplus$ or kept runs), dedup order ($\mathrm{supp}(N)\setminus R$ or
$\mathrm{supp}(N\setminus R)$), canonical layout, and whether holes own their
layouts or bind shared ones.

\subsection{Premises and scope}
\label{sec:incremental-scope}
Unproved premises: the probe and merge obligations of user types; purity of
Rust guards apart from $\bot$; adequacy of Rust resolution and ownership.
Differential dataflow uses $K=T\to_{fin}\mathbb Z$ with partially ordered
time and per-operator squares $\mathrm{acc}\circ impl=D\circ\mathrm{acc}$; it
places the annotation algebra in each structure's merger. Here it belongs to
$K$ and to $\mathsf{Runs}$. Extending to $\mathbb Z$ adds an annotation
parameter to structure leaves with addition and compaction; no structure
changes its abstraction.

\section{Physical: operators and read modes}
\label{sec:physical}

\paragraph{Grammar.} A complete standalone program.
\begin{lstlisting}
Physical ::= physical { Decl* Fact* Def* return [canon] [dedup] r as Head; }
Fact ::= consumer canonical;
Def ::= r = (pipe | let | exists) [canon] Op;
Op  ::= unit | rename R {..} [constants {source = literal, ...}]
      | select ... | point select ... | range select ...
      | bind ... | guard ... | project r keep {..} | merge [K] l, r
      | probe [first|smallest] [K] l, r
      | semi [eager|lazy] [K] l, r | anti [eager|lazy] [F] l, r
      | union [any|all] [fused] r1, r2, ... [BlockOrigin]
\end{lstlisting}
Bind retains RA's \code{bind r with [for] Pat [: Type] = (Expr)}
and unchanged Environment suffix: the optional token is semantic, not a read
mode. Generator Bind is streamed unnest, never an independent build target.

\paragraph{Contract.}
Write $D=(d_1,\ldots,d_n)$ for the definitions, $N$ for their names,
$h(r)=\operatorname{dom}A.rows[r].bindings$, and $o=t.returned$.
The functions $\operatorname{operands}$ and $\operatorname{ancestors}$ give
direct operands and their reflexive transitive closure.
$L$ is the set of logical names introduced by rename, bind and group;
$U(e,L)$ is the Rust expression's known uses in $L$.
$B(b)$ is the list of names introduced by a bind pattern.
$C(R)$ is the declared column list; $M(r)$ is a rename's source-to-target
mapping and $K(r)$ its literal-binding list. Sets of list entries below
forget order; cardinalities of lists retain duplicates.
$T_r$ is the map of logical type identities in $A.rows[r]$, restricted to
noncomputed columns. $\operatorname{bounds}(s)$ expands a between constraint
to two inclusive comparisons and orients comparisons with the column on
the left; it is undefined for any other constraint.
$\operatorname{closed}(s)$ is the set of literal and parameter terms of $s$;
$\operatorname{deps}(e,H)$ is the Rust free-variable dependency set in $H$.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-H-Definitions}{
 |\{R.name\mid R\in t.declarations\}|=|t.declarations|
 \quad\forall R\in t.declarations.\ |C(R)|>0\land|\operatorname{set}C(R)|=|C(R)|\\
 |N|=n\quad\forall i.\ \operatorname{operands}(d_i)\subseteq\{d_j.name\mid j<i\}\\
 \forall r=\operatorname{rename}(R)\in D.\ R\in t.declarations\land
 |\operatorname{dom}M(r)|=|M(r)|=|\operatorname{ran}M(r)|\land
 |\operatorname{sources}K(r)|=|K(r)|\land
 \operatorname{dom}M(r)\mathbin{\dot\cup}\operatorname{sources}K(r)=\operatorname{set}C(R)\\
 \forall r=\operatorname{rename}(R)\in D.\ K(r)\ne\bot\Rightarrow
 (|K(r)|>0\land\forall(c,v)\in K(r).\ v\in\operatorname{LeibnizLiterals})\\
 \forall b=\operatorname{bind}(s)\in D.\ |\operatorname{set}B(b)|=|B(b)|
 \land\operatorname{set}B(b)\cap h(s)=\varnothing\\
 \forall p=\operatorname{project}(s,Q)\in D.\ |Q|=|\operatorname{set}Q|\land\operatorname{set}Q\subseteq h(s)\\
 \forall d\in D,\ e\in\operatorname{rustPayloads}(d).\ d.environment=\bot\Rightarrow U(e,L)\subseteq h(d.input)\\
 \forall d\in D.\ d.environment\ne\bot\Rightarrow
 (|\operatorname{set}d.environment.variables|=|d.environment.variables|
 \land\operatorname{set}d.environment.variables\subseteq h(d.input))\\
 o\in N\quad N=\operatorname{ancestors}(o)
 \quad\forall e\in t.head.args.\ U(e,L)\subseteq h(o)}
 {\mathsf{PHDefinitions}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-H-Keys}{
 \forall d\in D_{\{probe,merge,semi,anti\}}.\ |d.key|=|\operatorname{set}d.key|\\
 \forall d\in D_{\{probe,merge,semi\}}.\ \operatorname{set}d.key=h(d.left)\cap h(d.right)\\
 \forall d\in D_{anti}.\ \operatorname{set}d.key\subseteq h(d.left)\cap h(d.right)\\
 \forall d\in D_{\{semi,anti\}}.\ h(d.name)=h(d.left)\\
 \forall d\in D_{\{probe,merge\}}.\ h(d.name)=h(d.left)\cup h(d.right)}
 {\mathsf{PHKeys}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-H-Range}{
 \forall s\in D_{range}.\ s.condition.equalities=\varnothing\land
 \exists c,B.\ \operatorname{bounds}(s.condition)=B\land |B|>0
 \land c\in\operatorname{dom}A.rows[s.input].types
 \land\forall(c',\theta,v)\in B.\
 c'=c\land\theta\in\{<,\le,>,\ge\}\land v\in\operatorname{closed}(s.condition)
 \land\operatorname{deps}(v,\operatorname{dom}A.rows[s.input].types)=\varnothing}
 {\mathsf{PHRange}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-H-Union}{
 \forall u\in D_{union}.\ |u.operands|\ge2\land
 \forall a,b\in u.operands.\ h(a)=h(b)\land
 \forall c\in\operatorname{dom}T_a\cap\operatorname{dom}T_b.\ T_a(c)=T_b(c)\\
 \forall u\in D_{union}.\ u.origin\ne\bot\Rightarrow
 \bigl(h(u.name)=\operatorname{set}(u.origin.free)\cup\operatorname{set}(u.origin.interface)
 \cup\!\!\bigcup_{p\in\operatorname{prefixes}(u.origin)}h(p)
 \land(u.origin.interface=\varnothing\iff u.witness\ne\bot)\bigr)\\
 \forall u\in D_{union}.\ (u.origin=\bot\land u.witness\ne\bot)\Rightarrow h(u.name)=\varnothing\\
 \forall u\in D_{union}.\ u.fused\Rightarrow
 \exists p.\ u.origin.over.prefix=p\land
 \forall a\in u.operands.\ a\ne p\land p\in\operatorname{ancestors}(a)}
 {\mathsf{PHUnion}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-H-Output}{
 (\forall e\in t.head.args.\ e\in\operatorname{BareVariables})\Rightarrow
 \bigcup_{e\in t.head.args}\operatorname{deps}(e,h(o))=h(o)}
 {\mathsf{PHOutput}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-H-Heading}{
 \mathsf{PHDefinitions}(t,A)\quad\mathsf{PHKeys}(t,A)\quad
 \mathsf{PHRange}(t,A)\quad\mathsf{PHUnion}(t,A)\quad\mathsf{PHOutput}(t,A)}
 {\mathsf{PHeading}(t,A)}
\end{mathpar}
Here $D_S$ selects definitions whose operator is in $S$,
$\operatorname{rustPayloads}$ selects bind expressions and guard conditions,
and $\operatorname{prefixes}$ returns the singleton recorded prefix or the
empty set. $\operatorname{LeibnizLiterals}$ is the literal set admitted by
the syntax equality checker. Range checking validates ordered comparison
syntax, not a Rust \code{Ord} implementation; that requirement is discharged
by generated code. Union checking here does not independently validate domain
metadata beyond its recorded prefix and heading.
The analysis derives rows, source identifiers, types, computed columns, hosts,
keyed/merge/range reads, scans, reader counts, modes and canon flags. With
$\mathsf{reads}(r)$ the operand and return uses of $r$:
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-Pipe}{\mathsf{mode}(r)=pipe\quad r\text{ is not a literal-bearing rename}\\
 \mathsf{reusableHost}(r)\ \lor\
 (\mathsf{linearOrFused}(r)\land r\notin\dom A.key\_reads)}
 {\mathsf{PModeOK}(r)}

\Rule{PH-Let}{\mathsf{mode}(r)\in\{let,exists\}\quad\mathsf{reads}(r)>0}
 {\mathsf{PModeOK}(r)}

\Rule{PH-Canon}{r=\mathsf{canon}(\ldots)\quad
 \mathsf{mode}(r)\in\{let,exists\}\ \lor\ (\mathsf{mode}(r)=pipe\land r\text{ is a host rename})}
 {\mathsf{PCanonOK}(r)}

\Rule{PH-Modes}{
 \forall r\in N.\ \mathsf{PModeOK}(r)\\
 \forall r\in N.\ r\in A.canon\Rightarrow\mathsf{PCanonOK}(r)\\
 \forall r\in N.\ \mathsf{mode}(r)=exists\Rightarrow h(r)=\varnothing}
 {\mathsf{PMode}(t,A)}

\Rule{PH-HeadDedup}{\mathsf{collapses}(H,\mathsf{heading}(r))\quad
 \neg\mathsf{consumerCanonical}\quad\mathsf{return}\ r\ \mathsf{as}\ H}
 {\code{return canon}\ \lor\ \code{return dedup}\text{ is recorded}}

\Rule{PH-Block}{\text{branch scopes derived from union origins and fixed operands}\\
 \text{Rust payloads and free-variable binding identities preserved}}
 {\mathsf{blockBindingsOK}(source,target)}

\Rule{WF-Physical}{\mathsf{PHeading}(t,A)\quad\mathsf{PMode}(t,A)}
 {\mathsf{WF}_{Physical}(t)\Downarrow A}
\end{mathpar}
A reusable host is a noncanonical host rename without literal bindings;
literal-bearing renames retain their filtered relation. Linear-or-fused means one read,
or only shared prefix reads inside a checked fused group. \code{exists} needs an
empty heading. \code{probe smallest} needs an adjacent admissible intersection
of $k\ge2$ providers, as specified by \textsc{PH-Smallest}.
Lazy semi/anti needs a reusable host read only lazily on that key. A witness
union (\code{any}) has an empty interface; a fused union names its prefix and
every arm depends on it. Redundant canon on a statically disjoint union is
permitted. The IX seam checks PH-CanonPlacement and IX demands independently.
$\mathsf{collapses}(H,K)$ holds when some head argument is computed and some
returned column is not a bare head argument: distinct valuations may then
yield one answer. The return-boundary dedup obligation is
\code{return canon} or \code{return dedup}; lowering records \code{dedup}
exactly when the head collapses and the return is not \code{canon}, and an
extra obligation is admissible. Structures types the recorded obligation
with the answer identity index (\code{r head}) and never recomputes it.

\paragraph{Canonical placement.}
Physical owns canonical boundaries; later stages consume their recorded flags.
Let $K_t(r)$ be the optional uniqueness key derived by one analysis of the
actual Physical program, its set inputs, and recorded boundaries.
Write $H(r)$ for its heading and $\bot$ for an unknown key. The following
transfers are shared by local checking and boundary placement; neither
key advice nor a second lowering analysis supplies premises.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-UniqueUnit}{r=\operatorname{unit}}{K_t(r)=\varnothing}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueJoin}{r=l\Join s\quad K_t(l)\ne\bot\quad K_t(s)\ne\bot}
 {K_t(r)=K_t(l)\cup K_t(s)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueAnti}{r=\operatorname{anti}(l,s)\ \lor\
 r=\operatorname{semi}(l,s)}{K_t(r)=K_t(l)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueFilter}{r=\operatorname{guard}(s)\ \lor\
 r=\operatorname{bind}_{scalar}(s)}{K_t(r)=K_t(s)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueSelect}{r=\sigma_\theta(s)\quad K_t(s)\ne\bot\quad
 C=\{c\mid(c=v)\in\theta,\ v\text{ is literal or query-closed}\}}
 {K_t(r)=K_t(s)\setminus C}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueGenerator}{r=\operatorname{bind}_{for}(s)}{K_t(r)=\bot}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueProject}{r=\pi_H(s)\quad K_t(s)\subseteq H}
 {K_t(r)=K_t(s)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueProjectDrop}{r=\pi_H(s)\quad
 K_t(s)=\bot\ \lor\ K_t(s)\not\subseteq H}{K_t(r)=\bot}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueUnion}{r=\bigcup_i s_i\quad
 \mathsf{operatorDisjoint}(r)\quad\forall i.\ K_t(s_i)\ne\bot}
 {K_t(r)=H(r)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueCanon}{r.\mathsf{canon}\quad K_t(r)=\bot}
 {K_t(r)=H(r)}
\and
\Rule{PH-UniqueGroup}{r=\operatorname{group}_{G}(s)}
 {K_t(r)=G\cap H(r)}
\end{mathpar}
PH-UniqueInput supplies the rename case: every input column is mapped or
constrained, so renaming an input set has its full output heading as key.
Union has no key unless PH-UniqueUnion applies; disjointness alone does not
prove uniqueness inside an arm. Capture keys are fixed host values, not columns.
Write $x\preceq_C y$ when every row of $x$ agrees on $C$ with a row of $y$.
The proof is reflexive and requires $C$ to remain in every intervening heading.
Selection, guard, scalar or generator bind, projection, semi and anti preserve
the input (left) relation; natural join preserves either operand; union
preserves a relation common to every arm. No lineage is inferred through group.
These are support-inclusion facts, independent of uniqueness.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-DisjointAnti}{
 \forall i<j.\ \exists a=\operatorname{anti}_C(l,b)\in D.\
 (s_i\preceq_C a\land s_j\preceq_C b)\ \lor\
 (s_j\preceq_C a\land s_i\preceq_C b)}
 {\mathsf{operatorDisjoint}(\bigcup_i s_i)}
\end{mathpar}
A fallback origin annotation alone proves nothing: the actual anti graph and
column-preserving paths must establish every pair's exclusion.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-CanonPlacement}{
 \forall g=\operatorname{group}(r,\ldots)\in D.\ K_t(r)\ne\bot\\
 K_t(o)\ne\bot\ \lor\ t.return\_canon\ \lor\ t.consumer\_canonical}
 {\mathsf{canonicalBoundaries}(t)\Downarrow\ok}
\end{mathpar}
This is an admissibility check, not a replay of default lowering: a missing
boundary is refused when duplicate valuations can reach a group or the
returned relation. Additional redundant canon, including on a disjoint union
or a return to a canonical consumer, is admissible subject to PH-Canon's
storage modes. Answer dedup for a collapsing computed head remains the
separate PH-HeadDedup obligation.

Default lowering invokes the shared Physical analysis on its operator graph.
It records binding-union boundaries unless the arms are operator-disjoint,
plus missing group-input and return boundaries, propagating each effective
boundary's key to later operators. A canonical consumer owns only the return.
The checker analyzes the submitted program, not the producer's choices.
No Sequence pass may remove a recorded boundary because a group follows it.

\paragraph{Smallest candidate intersection.}
Let $p$ be the outer valuation and $v$ one new column. An introducing probe
and its maximal adjacent chain of probe/semijoin membership checks read
providers $R_1,\ldots,R_k$ with headings $K_i\cup\{v\}$, where
$\varnothing\ne K_i\subseteq\mathsf{heading}(p)$ and
$v\notin\mathsf{heading}(p)$. Each provider is retained or a reusable host.
The introducing key is $K_1$; subsequent keys are $K_i\cup\{v\}$.
Every intermediate result is a noncanonical pipe, consumed by the next check.
The final result retains its written mode and canonicalization.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-Smallest}{k\ge2\quad C_i(p)=\{v\mid(p[K_i],v)\in R_i\}\\
 j=\mathop{\mathrm{arg\,min}}_{1\le i\le k}|C_i(p)|}
 {\bigcap_{i=1}^{k}C_i(p)
   =\{v\in C_j(p)\mid\forall i\ne j,\ v\in C_i(p)\}}

\Rule{PH-KeyProjection}{J=K\cup\{v\}\subseteq\mathsf{heading}(R)\quad
 R_J=\pi_J(R)\ \text{retained}\\
 R_J\text{ is private to one smallest membership edge}}
 {p\ltimes_J R=p\ltimes_J R_J}
\end{mathpar}
The opt-in \code{smallest_intersections} rewrite records this choice without
altering keys or Rust payloads. Explicit site advice may additionally
retain the exact key projection of a membership provider with extra payload,
by PH-KeyProjection. Later full reads still use the original provider.
The correspondence checker permits only these private projections, used once
by a selected smallest semijoin, and erases them back to the original named
graph. Ordinary empty/\code{all}/result selections do not introduce key views.
Independent interposed relational definitions may move before the entire chain in stable dependency order;
Rust nodes and dependencies on intermediate results block this exposure.
Empty/\code{all}/result-name advice retains its existing selection meaning.
Site advice \code{smallest(r_a,r_b,...)} names, by Physical definition name,
the rename of every provider of exactly one complete admissible site, reached
through unary relational provenance. Authored source advice
\code{smallest(R@a,S@b,...)} is resolved at the planning front: occurrence
$R@a$ to its first VO read step, then through the relational-lowering side
table to the rename's result name, which Physical keeps as its definition name.
Repeated, missing, partial, ambiguous or blocked sites are rejected, not widened.
The public Physical contract and rewrite correspondence check the result.
Selection uses only recorded columns and provenance, never Rust type inspection.
Structures supplies a candidate directory and full-key membership access for
every provider; native execution counts each set once and enumerates the minimum
(written-order ties) without allocating a candidate collection per valuation.
In an advised VO intersection, the first Fine subatom introduces $v$ and
subsequent adjacent subatoms check it; they do not independently bind $v$.
RL-Check correctly emits semijoins against their full occurrence relations.
PH-KeyProjection supplies candidate-only views for these semijoin sides
without losing payload correlation at later full expansions.


\paragraph{Retained streams.}
The opt-in Physical rewrite \code{cq_rewrites::retain_streams(r,...)} changes
only the mode of named stream results from \code{pipe} to \code{let}; operators,
keys and Rust steps are unchanged.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{EXT-RetainStream}{\mathsf{mode}(r)=pipe\quad r\text{ is not a host rename}}
 {\mathsf{mode}(r):=let\text{ preserves erasure and the exhaustive bag of }r}
\end{mathpar}
Under exhaustive pure-total evaluation the retained rows equal those of the
stream, so the rewrite is a refinement checked by \code{check_rewrite}. It is a
decomposition decision: a back end may start a new kernel or task at the
retained rows, so the consumer's parallel domain becomes those rows instead of
the stream's first relation.

\paragraph{Merges on retained sides.}
\code{cq_rewrites::merge_joins} replaces a selected probe by a merge only when
each side supplies order through retained storage, a reusable host, or a
same-key merge stream. A canon result with a single unkeyed reader is not
retained storage: PH-Canon stores it only in its canonical index, whose rows
move by value into that reader (STR-RowsByValue).
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{EXT-MergeRetained}{\mathsf{mode}(r)=let\quad\neg(r\in\mathrm{canon}\wedge
 r\text{ has one unkeyed reader})}
 {r\text{ may supply the order of a merge side}}
\end{mathpar}
Such a canon result is a canonical stream, as a canon pipe was; a merge on it
would add an ordered index the probe does not need. Modes are never changed.

\paragraph{Input sets.}
Every input relation is a set at the compiler boundary: its physical traversal
enumerates each complete row once. Producing that representation is the
IO/load layer's responsibility, by deduplicating or verifying complete rows
before query execution. It is not an optional compiler fact, context flag,
trusted partial key, or query-time certificate. Raw duplicate-bearing data
must cross this IO boundary before it is a relation input.
\begin{mathpar}
\Rule{PH-UniqueInput}{r=\mathsf{rename}(R)\quad
 R\in\mathsf{inputs}(t)\quad H=\mathsf{heading}(r)}
 {\mathsf{key}(r)=H}
\end{mathpar}
Rename maps or constrains every input column, so no varying input column is
lost. Joins combine operand keys; projection preserves a key only when it
retains every key column. A returned heading retaining the key needs no
\code{canon}; a projection dropping it still does. The input contract proves
only full-row uniqueness, never uniqueness on an arbitrary proper subset.
The current \code{smallest} intersection law denotes Set; bag/K-relation
semantics require a separate contract and are not admitted here.

\paragraph{Meaning.} Erasure to RA prime: probe and merge are joins, semi is a
semijoin, point/range are selections, modes and canon flags are dropped, union
and anti keep their origins. Context facts erase after their certificate or
discharge obligations; modes and choices change evaluation, not meaning.
Erasure retains the scalar/generator distinction, including in IX demand and
Physical rewrite certificates. Item multiplicity cannot justify scalar
uniqueness or literal inference.

\paragraph{Recorded decisions.} \code{pipe} (one streamed read), \code{let}
(retained, read more than once), \code{exists} (retained witness); \code{canon}
boundaries, stored in their canonical index (PH-Canon: lowering makes a canon
result a \code{let} unless it is a host rename whose canonical index is the
host carrier); \code{probe first|smallest}; \code{merge}; \code{semi/anti
eager|lazy}; \code{union any|all}, \code{fused} prefixes and dedup placement.
Defaults: written branch order, test unions \code{any}, binding unions
\code{canon} unless proved disjoint, fused prefixes for per-row arms. A fused
group streams its prefix once; fallback uses if-empty control flow and never
evaluates a later Rust arm after a witness.
In the preceding RA a natural join's left operand streams and its right
operand is indexed and probed; the default plan keeps written order, so the
first clause of each scope is the stream. Physical erasure remains exact; it does
not silently commute operands or invent demands.

\paragraph{Rewrite check (\code{cq_lang_physical::check_rewrite}).} Both programs
pass the contract; declarations, head, returned result and the definition graph
of named logical operators and operands are preserved; execution choices and
modes may change; recorded join and predicate placement decisions and
branch-scoped free-variable bindings are kept (PH-Block). It has no IX source;
the IX seam independently establishes final canonicality.

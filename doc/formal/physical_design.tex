\begin{abstract}
IX identifies required indexes and the index used at each relation-access
position. Selection produces Physical IR with external relation/index providers
and a lowering for each RA definition. Realization follows that fixed program
to Rust[Access], without selecting again. Hash and sorted maps are example
providers, not cases built into the proposed common compiler.
This note gives syntax, semantics, compilation rules, a mixed
relation fixture, and obligations for future zone-map pruning and trie access.
The driver and a borrowed exact-index instance are implemented; general stored
intermediates and block/YA realization are not. Push/pull placement remains open.
\end{abstract}

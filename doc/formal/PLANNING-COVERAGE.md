# Planning coverage from the notes to rules and code

This inventory covers notes 1.3 and 2.1–2.5 at the revision recorded in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](TERMINOLOGY.md). “Implemented” identifies a corresponding
algorithm; it does not discharge the Rust conformance or host-semantics
obligations. The complete rule-location inventory is [rules.tsv](rules.tsv).
The implemented physical alternative is documented in
[PHYSICAL-DESIGN.md](PHYSICAL-DESIGN.md) and [access_refinement.tex](access_refinement.tex).
Its 14 rules and two algorithms cover direct RA lowering, compiler-local resource
sharing and native Access operations. Exact reduction retains its residual after
optional block rejection. The [architecture discussion](architecture_placement.tex)
places choices at existing boundaries without a universal physical IR.

| Concept and note | Formalization and translation to RA | Implementation and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valuations, conjunction, and ordered answers ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | [foundations.tex](foundations.tex): valuation relations, conjunction-as-natural-join lemma, positional renaming, projection followed by ordered head rendering. P-Start/P-Combine/P-Output and B-* in [planning.tex](planning.tex) construct the corresponding operators. | Implemented by [rules.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs) and [staff_r1.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/staff_r1.rs). Correctness concerns complete sets under the stated host assumptions, not unrestricted Rust event traces. |
| Binary logical join plan ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | O-Leaf/O-Join annotate the frontier with a forest of binary trees. P-Combine replaces two trees by their binary parent and emits their natural join. | A proof annotation recoverable from occurrence coverage and the derivation; no new IR. The join-order tree differs from the join tree used for YA and from the complete RA dependency DAG containing semijoin references. |
| SIP adjacency and full shared-variable labels ([2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md)) | H-Adjacency derives an occurrence edge labeled by the entire nonempty intersection of its endpoint sorts. Constraint clauses contribute no SIP vertices. | [incidence](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational.rs) supplies occurrence sets; there is no separately constructed SIP-graph IR. The SIP graph alone neither chooses an order nor decides alpha-acyclicity: star and triangle hypergraphs can both have SIP graph K3. |
| Occurrence hypergraph and whole-tuple constraints ([2.3](../notes/04-the-variables-inside-the-picture.md)) | H-Occurrence/H-Incidence retain each atom's identity, ordered positional mapping, and variable set. P-Start emits a complete Rename. | Implemented by [incidence](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational.rs) and [introduce](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs). A ternary atom is never replaced by pairwise projections: without an additional join dependency that replacement can introduce false tuples. |
| Repeated relation occurrences, equal hyperedges, and reversed arguments ([2.3](../notes/04-the-variables-inside-the-picture.md)) | Distinct occurrence identities survive even when their sets are equal. H-Occurrence's mapping distinguishes `road(a,b)` from `road(b,a)`. | Implemented on the graph route. Repeating a variable *within* one atom, such as `road(x,x)`, instead uses prefix A-Repeat followed by an equality selection; it is outside D-Graph. See [clause_rules.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/clause_rules.rs). |
| Matched columns, new columns, Cartesian products, and joins that only filter ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | For sorts V,W, matched columns are V intersect W and new right columns are W minus V. P-Combine emits natural join in all cases. [physical.tex](physical.tex), M-JoinFor/M-JoinIf, distinguishes enumeration from membership. | Implemented in [materialized.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/physical/materialized.rs). Empty keys use scans/nonemptiness; no-new-column joins use an existence check. This case does not by itself mean the planner ran a YA semijoin sweep. |
| Constants and their graph marks ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md), [2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md), [2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | The SIP definition includes constant marks. D-Graph excludes constants; C-Constant introduces fresh typed literal bindings, and A-Constant/C-Match enforce matching through RA Extend/Rename/Join. | Implemented by [dependencies.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/dependencies.rs) and [clause_rules.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/clause_rules.rs). Literal normalization is a prefix administrative transition, not a separate graph vertex or nominal IR. |
| Selection and filter pushdown ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | P-Select requires every predicate variable in the chosen current sort and consumes that predicate once. Ready selections precede reduction, pruning, and combination; multi-occurrence predicates wait until their variables meet. | Implemented by [ready_selection/select](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs). The graph route admits only D-Move's primitive guards. General subtree placement of arbitrary host predicates is an abstract X-FilterLeft law in [constraint_planning.tex](constraint_planning.tex), not a current optimizer search. |
| Pruning at the last remaining use, including any completed subtree ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | K(C,P) retains head variables, crossing variables needed outside C, and pending predicate variables. P-Trim emits Project to exactly K; the retention lemma and global assembly invariant justify it. | Implemented by [retained_heading/trim](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs); independently checked by [planned_join.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/pass_contracts/planned_join.rs). Projection may merge witnesses under set semantics. It changes current result sorts, not the original query hypergraph. Prefix lowering retains witnesses until C-Finish because arbitrary Rust can observe their evaluation population. |
| Join trees, alpha-acyclicity, and GYO ([2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | H-JoinTree states running intersection. G-Private/G-Absorb and G-Accept/G-Reject construct or reject a certificate; termination and recognition are proved for the occurrence family. | Implemented by [hypergraph.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/hypergraph.rs), with independent tree checking and bounded exhaustive comparison against tree enumeration. Residual variable removal never deletes executable atoms. Empty original sets belong to the helper's broader domain; valid CQ graph inputs have positive-arity distinct-variable atoms. |
| Pairwise support versus complete support; YA reduction ([2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | Y-Init/Y-Up/Y-Down/Y-Freeze emit immutable semijoin versions along a checked join tree. Separator and subtree-support invariants prove a full reducer for the locally selected occurrence family. | Implemented by [rules.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs), scheduled in [cq_to_relational.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational.rs). Pending cross-occurrence predicates remain for assembly. The theorem does not cover them as already reduced constraints, or assert projected-output-linear complexity. |
| Connected, disconnected, and cyclic shapes ([2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | A disconnected acyclic hypergraph can have a join tree with empty separators. Those semijoins propagate emptiness; later natural joins become products. GYO rejection sends Auto to the binary greedy policy. | Implemented: disconnection is not cyclicity, and a cyclic query is not rejected merely for lacking a YA certificate. Full-support claims apply only to the acyclic route; cyclic pairwise support need not imply any complete answer. |
| Choosing an order versus checking its legality ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | Plan-Graph/Plan-Prefix dispatch by domain. S-Tree/S-Written/S-Connected/S-Greedy specify choices; O-* records the binary order. Auto grows a connected root coverage after YA; GreedyWidth can produce bushy trees. | Implemented in [strategy.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/strategy.rs). [planned_join.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/pass_contracts/planned_join.rs) verifies a legal complete derivation and its emitted RA, not the exact heuristic tie-breaks, canonical GYO choices, or BFS sequence. Connected order has no general cost-optimality guarantee. |
| Logical arity and a maximum-arity contract ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | [planning.tex](planning.tex) defines per-join union arity and w(D) over named RA results; E-Emit/E-Arity specify the emission cap. P-Trim lowers later sorts without erasing earlier peaks. | Implemented by [emit](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/rules.rs), the plan report, and the [independent checker](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/pass_contracts/planned_join.rs). Full occurrence Renames and semijoin versions count. Prefix lowering checks the completed module. Rejection does not establish that every possible plan exceeds the cap. |
| Cardinality, logical row-volume W0, and data-dependent order choices ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | [planning.tex](planning.tex) defines cardinality separately and W0 as the sum of raw binary-combine output cardinalities on an instance, before subsequent projection/filtering. | Semantic analysis and example measurement; no cardinality estimator or W0-minimizing policy is implemented. Semijoin probes, index lookups, duplicate input presentations, and convenience instrumentation are not W0. Neither W0 nor arity is a runtime-cost theorem. |
| Physical tuple bytes and graph-decomposition widths ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md), further directions in [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | The width discussion distinguishes logical arity, tuple cardinality, physical bytes, and decomposition widths. The notes themselves defer graph-decomposition widths. | No byte-width cost model, fractional-width optimizer, or WCOJ implementation. Concrete hash key/payload layouts are specified in [physical.tex](physical.tex); their existence does not supply one of those optimizers. |
| Arbitrary Rust expressions, directional requirements, and barriers (extension of the notes) | D-*/C-Ready distinguish binding availability from symmetric join equality. A-Prefix/A-Local place computed-key construction before the atom join or residual selection after candidates bind local arguments. [constraint_planning.tex](constraint_planning.tex) states the stronger pure X-* laws. | Current dependency and argument rules are implemented in [dependencies.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/dependencies.rs) and [clause_rules.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/cq_to_relational/clause_rules.rs). Shared variables may have alternative atom providers. A plain undirected hyperedge of all free variables cannot encode computation direction. General X-* subtree planning remains unimplemented. |
| From logical matches to concrete access ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | [physical.tex](physical.tex), IX-Result/IX-Base, uses the full shared sort as a key; ST-* and M-*/L-* explain exact key/complement layouts, scans, membership, and lookup. | Implemented by [relational_to_indexes.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/relational_to_indexes.rs) and the existing physical backends. Independently checked by [physical.rs](../../crates/cq-compiler/src/pass_contracts/physical.rs). This is the rule-to-RA-to-access connection, not a choice of the cheapest physical algorithm. |
| Union and other language extensions ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | Union in the notes explains broader named RA and combining query results. The current formal implementation domain is one CQ; negation/aggregation have no executable D-rule. | No Union operator in current RA. Negation/aggregation syntax parses, but their semantic/lowering contracts reject it pending the extension. These concepts must not be counted as implemented because they appear in the notes or parser. |

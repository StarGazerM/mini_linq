# Planning coverage from the notes to passes and code

This inventory covers notes 1.3 and 2.1–2.5 at the revision recorded in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](TERMINOLOGY.md). “Implemented” identifies a corresponding
algorithm; it does not discharge the Rust conformance or host-semantics
obligations. The formal TeX files describe the underlying logical ideas;
the implementation column identifies the current CQ-NF → VO → RA chain.
For physical choices after planning, see the
[IX section](ix_physical_contract.tex) and [DSL.md](../DSL.md).

| Concept and note | Formalization and translation to RA | Implementation and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valuations, conjunction, and ordered answers ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | [foundations.tex](foundations.tex) defines valuation relations, natural join, positional renaming, projection and ordered head rendering. A VO plan specifies the valuation-building steps; elaboration turns those steps into named RA and a final head projection. | [Normalization](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/normal.rs) creates CQ-NF; [ordering](../../crates/cq-order/src/lib.rs), [reduction](../../crates/cq-reduce/src/lib.rs) and [elaboration](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) preserve its `Kernel`. Correctness concerns complete sets under the stated host assumptions, not unrestricted Rust event traces. |
| Binary logical join plan ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | A Coarse VO plan has one full subatom per atom occurrence, so each later atom combines with the current valuation prefix. Fine splits atoms into variable-at-a-time subatoms. | [VO](../../crates/cq-ir/src/valuation.rs) records the plan; [elaboration](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) emits natural joins and partial-subatom checks. The valuation order differs from the join tree used for reduction and from the RA dependency DAG. |
| SIP adjacency and full shared-variable labels ([2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md)) | The shared-variable label of two atom occurrences is their full nonempty variable intersection. Guards and bindings contribute no SIP vertices. | [`cq::hypergraph::Analysis`](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) records clause variables and incidence. There is no separate SIP-graph IR. SIP adjacency alone neither chooses a VO order nor decides alpha-acyclicity: star and triangle bodies can both have SIP graph K3. |
| Occurrence hypergraph and whole-tuple constraints ([2.3](../notes/04-the-variables-inside-the-picture.md)) | Each atom keeps its clause identity, ordered positional mapping and entire variable set; a ternary tuple constraint is not replaced by pairwise witnesses. | [Normal form](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/normal.rs) makes atom arguments distinct variables, [analysis](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) records each occurrence, and [elaboration](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) renames the complete source relation at its first mention. |
| Repeated relation occurrences, equal hyperedges, and reversed arguments ([2.3](../notes/04-the-variables-inside-the-picture.md)) | Equal variable sets do not identify atom occurrences; positional mappings distinguish `road(a,b)` from `road(b,a)`. | VO subatoms carry clause indices, including for self-joins. Repeating a variable within an atom, as in `road(x,x)`, is normalized to a fresh atom variable plus an equality guard before ordering. |
| Matched columns, new columns, Cartesian products, and joins that only filter ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | For headings V and W, matched columns are V intersect W and new columns are W minus V. A natural join with no matched columns is a product; a partial subatom with no new columns tests existing valuations. | [Elaboration](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) emits natural joins or semijoins from VO steps. [IX analysis](../../crates/cq-index-policy/src/lib.rs) records demands, including empty-column demands; [physical lowering](../../crates/cq-physical-lowering/src/lib.rs) selects concrete operators later. |
| Constants and their graph marks ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md), [2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md), [2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | The note's SIP definition includes constant marks. In CQ-NF, a constant becomes a typed `let` binding supplying a fresh atom argument; matching remains part of the query kernel. | [`normalize`](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/normal.rs) places literal bindings before atoms. [Hypergraph analysis](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) uses atom variables, not constants as separate vertices. |
| Selection and filter pushdown ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | A guard step waits until its variables are bound. A guard over one atom can instead restrict that atom's relation before its first join. The pure movement laws in [constraint_planning.tex](constraint_planning.tex) describe a broader design space. | [`cq-order`](../../crates/cq-order/src/lib.rs) schedules ready guards; [`cq-reduce`](../../crates/cq-reduce/src/lib.rs) uses `Pushdown::Atoms` for single-atom guards and leaves opaque guards as steps. There is no general subtree search for arbitrary host predicates. |
| Pruning at the last remaining use, including any completed subtree ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | [planning.tex](planning.tex) describes why projected witnesses can merge under set semantics and how a retained heading can be justified. | Current [elaboration](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) projects the head at the end and projects a subatom to its selected columns when needed. The pass chain does not implement general last-use pruning over completed RA subtrees. |
| Join trees, alpha-acyclicity, and GYO ([2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | Running intersection characterizes a join tree; GYO removes private variables and contained residual edges to decide alpha-acyclicity. | [`cq::hypergraph::Analysis`](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) records GYO steps, acyclicity and the join tree when present. Residual edge removal never deletes an executable atom. |
| Pairwise support versus complete support; YA reduction ([2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | A full reducer semijoins along the join tree upward and downward, using all shared variables on each edge. Pairwise support alone is not complete support for a cyclic body. | [`cq-reduce`](../../crates/cq-reduce/src/lib.rs) inserts `Reductions::Tree` only for acyclic plans; [`cq-elaborate`](../../crates/cq-elaborate/src/lib.rs) emits semijoins over current clause relations. This does not assert projected-output-linear enumeration. |
| Connected, disconnected, and cyclic shapes ([2.2](../notes/04-the-picture-that-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | A disconnected acyclic body can have empty separators; a later natural join with no shared variables is a product. Cyclicity does not make the CQ invalid. | Analysis distinguishes acyclic from cyclic bodies. The facade's tree reduction is selected only when acyclic; cyclic bodies still proceed through ordering and elaboration without it. |
| Choosing an order versus checking its legality ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | VO makes the valuation order explicit: Written, Prefix with Connected/GreedyWidth/Auto ranking, or Elimination; Coarse and Fine set its granularity. | [`cq-order`](../../crates/cq-order/src/lib.rs) compiles the selected options and checks the resulting VO contract and correspondence. [Analysis](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) supplies the elimination order. These heuristics make no cost-optimality guarantee. |
| Logical arity and a maximum-arity contract ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md)) | [planning.tex](planning.tex) distinguishes heading arity of named RA results from cardinality and describes a formal arity cap. | Current [hypergraph analysis](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/hypergraph.rs) records elimination width plus one, a different measure. Current [VO](../../crates/cq-ir/src/valuation.rs) and [RA](../../crates/cq-ir/src/relational_plan.rs) contracts validate headings but do not expose a maximum-arity rejection option. |
| Cardinality, logical row-volume W0, and data-dependent order choices ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | [planning.tex](planning.tex) defines cardinality separately and W0 as a sum of raw binary-combine output cardinalities on an instance. | These are analysis and example measures; no cardinality estimator or W0-minimizing order option is implemented. Neither W0 nor heading arity is a runtime-cost theorem. |
| Physical tuple bytes and graph-decomposition widths ([2.4](../notes/05-the-variables-the-plan-still-needed.md), further directions in [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | The notes distinguish logical arity, tuple cardinality, physical bytes and decomposition widths. | No byte-width cost model, fractional-width optimizer or WCOJ performance claim is established here. Fine VO plans provide variable-at-a-time generic join steps; the Structures language binds reads to host keys or built types ([DSL.md](../DSL.md)). |
| Arbitrary Rust expressions, directional requirements, and barriers (extension of the notes) | A computed atom argument becomes a `let` binding or `if` guard in CQ-NF. VO requires inputs to be bound before the step that uses them; [constraint_planning.tex](constraint_planning.tex) states broader pure movement laws. | [Normal form](../../crates/cq-ir/src/cq/normal.rs) performs the transformation, [ordering](../../crates/cq-order/src/lib.rs) observes binding readiness, and [reduction](../../crates/cq-reduce/src/lib.rs) leaves opaque guards in place. A plain undirected hyperedge of all free variables cannot encode computation direction. |
| From logical matches to concrete access ([2.1](../notes/04-the-order-logic-did-not-choose.md), [2.5](../notes/06-the-order-the-compiler-chose.md)) | [ix_physical_contract.tex](ix_physical_contract.tex) gives RA → IX, preserving exact index demands; Physical and Structures then fix operators and reads ([DSL.md](../DSL.md)). | [Index analysis](../../crates/cq-index-policy/src/lib.rs), [physical lowering](../../crates/cq-physical-lowering/src/lib.rs), [structure selection](../../crates/cq-structure-selection/src/lib.rs) and the [printer](../../crates/cq-physical-realization/src/lib.rs) implement these boundaries; the [full-chain test](../../crates/mini-linq/tests/physical_query.rs) compares supported output with an independent oracle. |
| Union and other language extensions ([1.3](../notes/03-the-plan-that-meant-the-same-thing.md)) | Union in the notes explains broader named RA and combining query results. The current normal-form domain is one positive CQ with guards and bindings. | Current RA has no Union operator. Negation and aggregation syntax parse, but the normal-form contract rejects them pending a semantic and lowering contract. |

Evidence includes the [normal-form and VO contracts](../../crates/cq-ir/tests/valuation_language.rs),
[hypergraph tests](../../crates/cq-ir/tests/hypergraph_analysis.rs),
[ordering tests](../../crates/cq-order/tests/order.rs),
[reduction tests over the shared cases](../../crates/cq-reduce/tests/reduce.rs),
[elaboration tests](../../crates/cq-elaborate/tests/elaborate.rs), and the
separate [full-chain test](../../crates/mini-linq/tests/physical_query.rs).
Normalization checks the CQ-NF contract; the order, reduction and elaboration
passes check their targets and source/target correspondence, with options for
order and reduction. VO and RA kernels agree with the CQ-NF kernel up to
renaming. Logical oracle checks do not certify physical execution of every
authored plan. See
[evaluation.tex](evaluation.tex) for physical validation boundaries and
[foundations.tex](foundations.tex) for the semantic assumptions.

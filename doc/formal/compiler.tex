\begin{abstract}
MiniLinq compiles conjunctive queries with embedded Rust into Rust iterator
programs through six complete intermediate languages: CQ, VO, RA prime, IX,
Physical and Structures. All share one denotation, Kleisli composition over
$\bot+\mathrm{Set}$ against the written order. For each language we give its
grammar, the invariants its local contract checks, its meaning and the
decisions it records. For each pass we give its compilation rules, the
structural correspondence check that is run independently of the pass, and
the refinement statement that check supports. Checks are ordinary structural
contracts: no search and no equivalence decision.
\end{abstract}

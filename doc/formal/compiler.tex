\begin{abstract}
MiniLinq compiles conjunctive queries through named relational algebra and IX,
which records both required indexes and explicit references from relation-access
positions. A replaceable policy produces Physical IR, recording individual
relation/index providers and the lowering of each RA definition. Realization
follows these fixed choices without rerunning selection. The proposed common
compiler has no closed catalogue of data structures or global relation-implementation
switch. We specify both boundaries with syntax, semantics, algorithmic inference
rules and authored expectations for heterogeneous relation access.
The generic driver and a borrowed exact-index instance are implemented and
checked against real macro expansion. General stored intermediates and
block/YA realization remain outside the implemented domain.
The relational planning theory is retained; the placement of push/pull and
lazy-to-eager transformations remains open.
\end{abstract}

# Paired benchmark, 2026-11-02/03: `44bf82bc` (current) vs `c1b75718` (reference)

Raw samples, per-item CIs and provenance:
[`bench-2026-11-02.results.json`](bench-2026-11-02.results.json).

**Reference.** `c1b75718` (2026-10-31, "DOOP relational heads") is the last
commit with a full timing anchor in
[PLANNING-REDESIGN](../../../doc/PLANNING-REDESIGN.md) (P3.3, P3.3-lite/M0 and C7Provider
entries). Later entries time only subsets against `c395fe83`, `17a14b93` and
`20101707`, so none of them is a full anchor.

**Protocol.** Each sample is a fresh process. There are 5 interleaved pairs, with the
order reversed on odd pairs. Every sample's answer is checked for exactness.
The ratio is median(current)/median(reference). Its CI is a paired bootstrap
over all 5^5 resamples, percentile 95%. Suite geometric means resample each item
independently (20,000 draws). Measurements held the timing gate and the exclusive
timing lock (`tools/README.md`), with no profiler. Each sample records whole-process
minor and major faults, max RSS, loadavg, CPU MHz, the governor (`schedutil`) and
the nvidia-smi tenants. Host: 24 CPUs. Other agents' builds were running during the
CPU suites (loadavg per sample 2.3–9.2, median 3.3). A foreign GPU tenant ran
throughout (user `chan`, `VLLM::EngineCore`, 45 GB VRAM, about 1 core).
It doesn't affect the CPU suites, but it blocks local GPU timing.

## GPU suites: pending RunPod session

Local GPU timing was blocked for the whole window by the foreign vLLM tenant. The
driver waited for two tenant-free checks 60 s apart. One attempt started in a gap
and aborted before its first sample when the tenant returned; it is recorded in
`aborted_attempts`. The GPU half runs on a disposable RTX 6000 Ada pod
(`tools/remote_gpu`). It uses bundles of both revisions and
[`pod_bench_2026_11_02.py`](pod_bench_2026_11_02.py), which interleaves GPU
TPC-H advised SF1 and SF10 (all queries exact) and `doop-gpu` xalan/batik
(26/26 exact against `doop-mini` once per bundle, with counts checked every sample).
Limitations of the pod run: there's no live SRDatalog (no SRD toolchain on the
pod), and no `srd-doop-gpu`, because the SRD prepared corpus isn't on the
volume. Locally built SRDatalog field-flow libraries (fab849f, baseline plans,
CUDA 12.9) are under `/tmp/bench-1102/srd-work`. Until the pod session lands,
the recorded Oct 26 SRD fullclock ranges stay historical and are not replaced.
